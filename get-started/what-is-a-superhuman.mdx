## Definition

A **Superhuman™ Teammate** works a revenue role on your behalf. In the console,
each Teammate is a **Superhuman** object. It owns the **Brain** (its core
information, instructions, and behavior) and the knowledge it's allowed to use,
and it can power many [Deployments](/get-started/superhumans-vs-deployments) at once.

You extend what a Superhuman can do with **Sub-Agents** and **Skills**. See
[The five layers](/get-started/five-layers).

## Roles

Superhumans map to revenue roles, for example:

- **Website Seller** — qualifies inbound, runs the demo, books the meeting
- **Inbound SDR** — instant, context-aware follow-up on every hand-raise
- **Solutions Engineer** — technical depth, demos, and deal-room coverage
- **Customer Success** — onboarding, retention, and expansion at scale
- **PLG Guide** — in-product activation coaching
- **Ride-Along** — joins live calls alongside your rep
