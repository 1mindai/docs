## Overview

1mind is a **Superhuman™ Teammate System** that deploys GTM Superhuman™ Teammates
for revenue teams. Each Superhuman's GTM brain is made up of an AI Knowledge Graph
and an Action Orchestration layer, built to work across the entire customer
lifecycle. Superhumans do the work of SDRs, first-call AEs, SEs, CSMs, and
In-Product Guides (a previously non-existent role).

Superhuman™ Teammates integrate into existing workflows to qualify buyers, book
meetings, deliver pitches, give demos, handle objections, uncover pain points,
build value models, and onboard customers. Each Teammate draws on your knowledge,
follows your rules, and completes the task in one conversation, then gives your
team the full context.

## How 1mind is organized

Everything is built on a shared object model:

**Account → Workspace → Superhuman → Deployment**

- A **Workspace** contains your Superhumans, knowledge, personas, and integrations.
- A **Superhuman** is the console object behind each Teammate. It holds the Brain and enabled knowledge.
- A **Deployment** is a specific customer-facing experience for a channel or campaign, powered by a Superhuman.

The product is layered — see [The five layers](/get-started/five-layers) for how
the platform, roles, surfaces, sub-agents, and skills stack together.
