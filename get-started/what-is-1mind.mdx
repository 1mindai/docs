## Overview

1mind is an AI engagement platform for revenue teams. Its **Superhumans** are AI
agents that engage buyers in real time — qualifying inbound, running demos,
routing to the right rep, booking meetings, and supporting customers — across
three surfaces: your **website**, inside your **product**, and on **live calls**.

Rather than a chatbot that hands off, a Superhuman carries the full conversation:
it draws on your knowledge, follows your rules, and completes the task in one flow.

## How 1mind is organized

Everything is built on a shared object model:

**Account → Workspace → Superhuman → Deployment**

- A **Workspace** contains your Superhumans, knowledge, personas, and integrations.
- A **Superhuman** is the shared AI configuration (its Brain and enabled knowledge).
- A **Deployment** is a specific customer-facing experience for a channel or campaign, powered by a Superhuman.

The product is layered — see [The five layers](/get-started/five-layers) for how
the platform, roles, surfaces, sub-agents, and skills stack together.
