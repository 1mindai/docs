## The distinction

A **Superhuman** is the shared configuration — the Brain and enabled knowledge.
A **Deployment** is a specific customer-facing experience for a particular
channel, campaign, or surface, powered by that Superhuman.

The same Superhuman can run many deployments. The Brain and knowledge stay shared
at the Superhuman level, while each deployment can have its own persona, surface,
experience settings, context, campaign tracking, and launch state.

| | Superhuman | Deployment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Shared AI config (Brain + knowledge) | A configured customer-facing experience |
| Scope | One per role/expertise | One or many per Superhuman |
| Owns | Instructions, behavior, enabled knowledge | Persona, surface, experience, tracking, launch settings |

<Note>
  The name shown to a visitor comes from the **Persona** selected on the
  deployment, not from the Superhuman's internal name.
</Note>
