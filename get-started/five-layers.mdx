Every Superhuman is built from five layers. The Platform is always on; you choose
who works, how they show up, and how far they go.

| Layer | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform** | The always-on intelligence layer — Context Graph, Knowledge Hub, attribution, and enterprise security, RBAC, and workspaces. |
| **Superhumans** | The unit you buy — the role that works (Website Seller, Inbound SDR, Solutions Engineer, and more). Each role is a line item. |
| **Deployments** | How a Superhuman shows up — the surfaces: avatar, voice, brand character, and text/chat, across sandbox and production. |
| **Sub-Agents** | Tier-gated capabilities that extend what a Superhuman can do (e.g., Calendar & Routing, Live Chat Handoff, Live Demo). |
| **Skills** | Modular add-ons you can attach to any Superhuman (e.g., ROI Calculator, Artifacts & Recaps, Stripe Checkout). |
