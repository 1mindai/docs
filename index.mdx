1mind is a **Superhuman™ Teammate System** that deploys GTM Superhuman™ Teammates
for revenue teams within Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success. Teammates qualify
buyers, deliver pitches, give demos, book meetings, and onboard customers on your
website, inside your product, and on live calls. Our additional modalities and
capabilities turn your entire website into an AI asset.
