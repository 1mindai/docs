A Deployment is how a Superhuman shows up for a specific channel or campaign.
Surfaces and controls include:

- **Avatar**: the photorealistic face for high-consideration moments
- **Voice**: voice-first mode
- **Brand character**: a custom illustrated character tuned to your brand
- **Text & Chat**: the lightest surface, same brain and knowledge
- **Channels**: embed, widget, ride-along
- **Sandbox & Production**: safe promotion with rollback
- **A/B Testing**: split real traffic across variants

<Note>See [Superhumans vs Deployments](/get-started/superhumans-vs-deployments) for how one Superhuman powers many deployments.</Note>
