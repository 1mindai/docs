A **deployment** is a configured instance of a Superhuman — same brain, knowledge,
and integrations, wrapped in its own appearance, greeting, colors/fonts, widget or
embed configuration, privacy policy, suggested topics, UTM tracking, and launcher
script. It's the presentation layer.

Every deployment has two states: **Sandbox** (safe editing; changes isolated) and
**Production** (live; tabs become read-only after promotion).

<Note>Rule of thumb: same role + same knowledge base = one Superhuman, add a deployment. If either differs, create a new Superhuman.</Note>

## Prerequisites

- Access to your 1mind Console (`app.1mind.com`) with permission to create/manage deployments.
- The Superhuman already created (its Brain and knowledge configured).

## 1. Create the deployment

In the Console, select the **Superhuman**, click **Deployments** → **Create New Deployment**.

## 2. Basics tab

**About**

- **Deployment name** — use a clear convention: `[Customer] [Use case] [Widget/Embed]` (e.g., "LaunchDarkly Website BDR Widget").
- **Persona** — select the persona for this deployment.

**Preferences**

- **Allowed domains** — whitelist the domains this deployment can run on, or `*` for all.
- **Remember user** — remember returning users across sessions.
- **Page context** — let the Superhuman know where the visitor is on the site.
- **ZoomInfo enrichment** — toggle per your requirements.

## 3. Experience Builder — styling & content

<Tip>
  Before styling, gather the customer's brand colors and fonts (from their site or
  brand kit).
</Tip>

**Layout**

- **Display** — Embedded or Widget.
- **Embedded landing layout** — Descriptive or Minimal.
- **Widget template** — Full, Circular, Compact, In-Product, and more.
- **Widget size & positioning** — dimensions and placement (left/right).

**Appearance**

- **UI theme** — Dark or Light.
- **Font** — 25+ options to match the brand.
- **Primary colors** — three values for gradient control.
- **Custom border gradient** — to complement the palette.
- **Brand Kit overrides (advanced):** surface background, panel background, AI chat bubble color, AI chat text color, outer glow.

**Content**

- **Display name, heading, sub-heading, CTA button text** for the widget/embed.
- **Loader messages** — shown while the Superhuman loads.
- **Intro message** — the initial greeting the user sees.

## 4. Experience Builder — behavior

- **Privacy policy** — enter your customer-specific privacy policy text (widget and/or in-experience/persistent disclaimer, with optional dismissal).
- **Action buttons** — next steps the Superhuman offers mid-conversation (book a meeting, open a demo, send a download). Each deployment has its own set.
- **Suggested topics** — conversation-starter chips that guide the conversation.
- **Session settings** — display mode, inactivity/warning/idle timers (defaults are usually fine).
- **Language & greeting** — off by default. Enable **Multilingual support** if needed (greeting must be Dynamic when on). **Greeting override** lets you replace the trained greeting (Static = exact phrase; Dynamic = instructions).

## 5. UTM tracking

- **Campaign** and **placement** tracking parameters if you use UTMs; preview the reporting filters to confirm.
- **Outbound link tracking** — a per-deployment toggle that appends a unique deployment identifier to every outbound link the Superhuman shares (solves multi-team attribution when several deployments share one Superhuman).

## 6. Launcher codes

The **Launcher Codes** tab (auto-activates the deployment) gives you:

- **Deployment code** — the unique identifier.
- **Launcher script** — the JavaScript embed for your website (see [Website embed & script installation](/configuring/integrations/website-embed)).
- **Testing link** — a live preview URL, no HTML test page needed.

## 7. Test

Open the **Testing link** and verify: branding (colors, fonts, theme), widget
template/position, privacy policy shows your name (not "1mind"), chat works and
history is enabled, welcome message displays, and action buttons/suggested topics
behave as configured. Adjust in the relevant tab and re-test as needed.

## 8. Promote to production

On the **Launcher Codes** tab, click **Promote to Production** to move from Sandbox
to live.

<Warning>
  Promoting a deployment does **not** promote the Superhuman's Brain — that's a
  separate release. Production tabs are read-only; to iterate, edit in Sandbox,
  test, and promote again.
</Warning>
