A custom **Face & Voice** gives your Superhuman a tailored on-brand appearance and
voice. Creating one is a collaboration between you and the 1mind Services design
team, in three phases.

## What to decide at kickoff

At the kickoff, confirm two things up front to keep the project on track:

- **Decision-makers and sign-off.** Who on your side approves the design, and what
  level of sign-off is required? Aligning this early prevents delays at the
  approval stages.
- **Scope and iterations.** You'll get **up to 3 rounds of feedback** on the design
  options — good to set expectations here so scope stays clear.

You'll also walk through the available customization options so you can design a
Superhuman you're happy with.

## What you provide

Your core inputs (brand direction and preferences) via the intake form your CSM
shares. From there, 1mind can either build a fully custom Face & Voice or start
from a pre-built option in the library, depending on your request.

## The process at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Kickoff & intake">
    We introduce the Face & Voice options; you confirm decision-makers and sign-off,
    then submit your inputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design options">
    Services design shares a **contact sheet of 6 design options** based on your inputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & feedback">
    You review the options with your CSM (usually on a call, with a designer present)
    and give up to **3 rounds of feedback**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design sign-off">
    You give formal approval on the final design to move into production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production & delivery">
    1mind produces the Superhuman, adds it to your console, and shares a **testing
    link** for your review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your QA & final revisions">
    You test and give feedback; Services makes the final revisions and delivers the
    completed Face & Voice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key expectations

- **Up to 3 feedback rounds** on the design options — confirmed at kickoff to keep scope clear.
- **Confirm decision-makers early** so approvals don't stall the timeline.
