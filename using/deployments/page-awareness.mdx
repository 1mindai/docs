## What page awareness is

Page awareness lets a single deployment, running on every page of your site, vary parts of its experience from one page to another. It is the supported way to make one page differ from another without adding a second deployment, which a page cannot carry (see [Website embed](/configuring/integrations/website-embed)).

## When page awareness does not fit

If pages need genuinely different setups, an embed on one page and a widget on another, or different personas or flows per page, keep each deployment on its own pages instead. See [Giving different pages a different experience](/configuring/integrations/website-embed#giving-different-pages-a-different-experience).
