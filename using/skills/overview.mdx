<Info>
  **Overview page.** Skills are modular add-ons. Each page notes its availability
  and the tier/entitlement it requires — confirm with Product before publish.
</Info>

Skills add specific capabilities to any Superhuman. Planned pages:

- **SMB Closer** — configure, price, quote, and pay in one conversation
- **Artifacts & Recaps** — leave-behinds written from the actual conversation
- **ROI Calculator** — builds the buyer's business case live
- **Code Generation** — integration snippets and RFP file responses on demand
- **Stripe Checkout** — checkout and CPQ quoting against your pricing rules

<Note>See [The five layers](/get-started/five-layers) for how Skills differ from Sub-Agents.</Note>
