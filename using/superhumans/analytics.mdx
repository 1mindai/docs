**Analytics** gives an aggregate view of how people use and progress through one Superhuman. The page is titled **Performance Overview** and organizes results into engagement, conversion, duration, and usage-and-experience sections. For individual records use [Engagement: Conversation Explorer](/using/superhumans/engagement); for quality findings use [Scorecard](/using/superhumans/scorecard). These pages use different reporting populations, so their totals may not match.

## Set the reporting scope

Open the Superhuman, select **Analytics**, choose **Sandbox** or **Production**, and set the scope before interpreting results:

- **Date range** — 7d, 30d, 90d, All, or Custom (select start/end dates, then **Show Results**).
- **Deployment** — the deployment or combined scope from the dropdown.

Use the same environment, date range, and deployment scope when comparing metrics — a comparison is not meaningful when one control differs.

## Engagement Funnel (Sessions)

Stage-to-stage rates over sessions (a visitor can contribute multiple sessions):

- **Start Rate** — started ÷ viewed sessions.
- **Engagement Rate** — engaged ÷ started sessions (engaged = the visitor replied at least once).
- **Meaningful Rate** — sessions reaching the Meaningful threshold ÷ engaged sessions.

Read them in order and identify the largest drop-off, then open Conversation Explorer with a comparable scope.

## Conversion Funnel (Unique Users)

Four stages by unique visitor identifier: **Viewed → Started → Engaged → Meaningful**. Interpret as unique visitors, not authenticated people — a person on a new browser/device can appear as another visitor. A visitor seen in both Embedded and Widget is deduplicated in the combined total, so the combined number can be lower than the sum of the two placements.

<Note>The two funnels are not expected to match: the Engagement Funnel counts sessions; the Conversion Funnel deduplicates unique visitors.</Note>

## Duration, usage, and experience

- **Average Duration** — average active conversation time for engaged sessions (an active-conversation measure, not wall-clock time). The portal shows a readable duration; exports record seconds.
- **Repeat Engagement** — a return-behavior indicator for the active scope.
- **Primary Mode Split** — the Voice-versus-Chat classification.

## Export

The download creates an **XLSX Analytics Export** for one Superhuman and up to 90 trailing days, with four worksheets: **Daily Performance**, **Daily Performance by Mode** (Embedded/Widget), **Daily Performance by Page** (with campaign values when available), and **Metadata** (definitions and formulas). It contains aggregate metrics, not transcripts. If today is included, it holds the current partial day — export through yesterday for complete-day comparisons. Review the **Metadata** sheet before combining with another report.

## When a card is empty

Confirm the environment, date range, and deployment filter; clear an overly narrow custom range; try **All** if appropriate; check Conversation Explorer for records in a comparable scope; and allow time for reporting processing, then reload. An empty card is not proof that no activity occurred.
