The 1mind Ride-Along Superhuman is an AI sales engineer that joins your live calls
as a full participant: on camera, with a voice, and with deep knowledge of your
product. It listens to the whole room, watches what is shared on screen, and
speaks when your team calls on it to handle technical questions, security
objections, and product deep dives — everywhere your customers meet.

## What's new

This page replaces the earlier Zoom App documentation. The Ride-Along platform has
been rebuilt and expanded:

- **Three platforms** — Google Meet and Microsoft Teams join Zoom as fully supported platforms. One Superhuman, every meeting link.
- **Calendar scheduling** — Your Superhuman has its own email address. Add it to a calendar invite and it RSVPs, reminds you, and joins on time.
- **Screen understanding** — When anyone shares a screen, the Superhuman sees it and can speak to exactly what is being shown.
- **Multi-speaker hearing** — Every participant is heard as a distinct voice.
- **Meeting chat Q&A** — Attendees ask questions in the meeting chat and get expert answers without interrupting the conversation.
- **Multilingual calls (Beta)** — Pick the language for each meeting; disclosures and chat replies are translated automatically.
- **CRM briefings** — Attach the Salesforce or HubSpot account, opportunity, and contacts so it walks in knowing the deal.
- **A richer console** — Upcoming, Live, and History tabs with live statuses, co-hosts, invitees, briefing sheets, and summaries.

<Note>
  **No app install required.** The Superhuman joins as a standard meeting
  participant on all three platforms. There is nothing for you, your team, or your
  customers to download, install, or authorize in the meeting itself.
</Note>

## What the Ride-Along Superhuman does

- **Expert backup on every call** — Precise answers on security, architecture, integrations, and product capabilities, delivered the moment the question is asked.
- **A polite participant, not a wildcard** — By default it joins silently and speaks only when invited. Your team decides, per meeting and even mid-meeting, how forward it should be.
- **Aware of the whole meeting** — It hears each speaker as a distinct voice, sees shared screens, reads the meeting chat, and carries the briefing you gave it.
- **An active presenter when you want one** — It can share slides, play product videos, and walk through live web content, while you keep hold-and-stop controls in the console.

## Platforms: Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams

The Superhuman joins on all three platforms with the same core experience: a
named, on-camera participant that posts its AI disclosure in the chat as it
arrives and behaves according to the engagement mode you chose. Capabilities are
consistent across platforms, with one exception: private (direct) chat messages
are a Zoom-only feature, so host welcome messages and private replies fall back to
the public meeting chat elsewhere.

| Capability | Zoom | Google Meet | Microsoft Teams |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Join instantly by meeting link | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Join from a calendar invite | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| On-camera presence with live avatar | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Hears each speaker separately | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Sees shared screens | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Presents slides, video, and live web content | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI disclosure and replies in meeting chat | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Answers attendee questions in chat (Chat Q&A) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Private messages to hosts | Zoom only | Public chat | Public chat |
| Waits politely in waiting rooms and lobbies | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Meeting links are recognized automatically, including links embedded in calendar
invites (`zoom.us`, `meet.google.com`, `teams.microsoft.com`, and `teams.live.com`).

### Admission works like any external guest

The Superhuman enters through the front door. Zoom waiting rooms, Microsoft Teams
lobbies, and Google Meet's "ask to join" flow all behave normally: the host admits
it once and it joins. If your organization restricts guest or external
participants, those platform policies apply to the Superhuman too — confirm them
with your meeting-platform admin before an important call.

## Two ways to bring your Superhuman to a meeting

### Instant: paste a meeting link

From the **Conferencing** page in your 1mind console, choose **Add to meeting**,
paste any Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams link, and it joins in seconds.
Before it joins you can name the meeting and set the engagement mode, add context
notes and attach files, pick the meeting language (when multilingual is enabled),
and add co-hosts and toggle chat Q&A.

### Scheduled: invite it like a teammate

Every Ride-Along Superhuman has its own email address, shown at the top of your
Conferencing page (for example `superhuman-a1b2c3@1mind.app`, or a friendly alias
on your own domain). Add that address to a calendar event, or forward the invite
to it, and:

- It replies to the invite, so Accepted or Declined shows in your calendar.
- The meeting appears in your **Upcoming** tab, ready to be briefed.
- The organizer gets a reminder email shortly before start.
- It joins on time, in the mode you set.

**Scheduling details worth knowing:**

- **Whoever forwards the invite owns the meeting** in the console and is automatically added as a host.
- **Recurring meetings are supported** (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), including edited or cancelled single occurrences. You can also forward just one occurrence.
- **Reschedules and cancellations sync.** Move or cancel the event and the Superhuman moves or bows out with it.
- **All-day events are not supported.** If an invite can't be scheduled (no link, unsupported link, past event), you get an email explaining why.

## Engagement modes

Each meeting has an engagement mode, set before the call and changeable at any
moment during it. Mode changes apply instantly and are attributed in the meeting
record.

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Silent** (default) | Won't speak at all. Still listens, watches shared screens, and can answer in the meeting chat if chat Q&A is on. The safest setting. |
| **Standby** | Listens until activated by a wake word. Say its name to have it weigh in; it returns to listening afterward. Available on request for your workspace. |
| **Active** | Actively participates, no wake words required. Jumps in on its own when it can add value. You can give it an opening introduction to deliver when it joins. |

**Four ways to switch modes mid-call:** one click on the live meeting screen in
the console; chat commands (`/active`, `/standby`, `/silent`); natural language in
chat ("you can jump in now"); or wake words in Standby.

## Brief it like you would a colleague

Open any upcoming meeting to see its briefing sheet. Most of this can also be set
when you create an instant meeting, and adjusted while the call is live.

- **Context notes** — Free-form guidance for this session: goals, product focus, competitive landscape, landmines to avoid.
- **Files and documents** — Attach decks, one-pagers, pricing sheets, and docs (PDF, PowerPoint, Word, text, images, video; up to 25 MB per file, 100 files per meeting). A readiness indicator confirms when files are processed. Files can be added or removed mid-meeting.
- **CRM context (Salesforce and HubSpot)** — Associate the account, opportunity, and contacts. For scheduled meetings, a research pass runs automatically about 30 minutes before start, with your operator reviewing what the AI receives.
- **Meeting language (Beta)** — Choose from the languages your Superhuman persona speaks; the disclosure, chat replies, and voice follow the selection.
- **Co-hosts** — Add teammates as hosts so they can switch modes, send instructions, upload documents, and message it privately.
- **Who's invited** — For calendar-scheduled meetings, the attendee list and roles are read from the invite.

## What it can do in the meeting

**It hears the room.** Audio is processed per participant in real time, so it
follows multi-person conversations naturally.

**It sees what is shared.** When anyone presents, it watches the screen live and
can speak to exactly what's on screen right now.

**It can present, too.** It can share slide decks, play product videos, and walk
through live web content. From the console, the organizer keeps control: **Hold
content** pins the current slide, and **Stop sharing** ends the share immediately.

**The meeting chat is a second channel.** With chat Q&A enabled, attendees get
expert answers in chat without interrupting the speaker. The chat brain shares full
context with the voice brain. Host commands:

| In the chat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/active` `/standby` `/silent` | Switches the engagement mode (hosts only, with a confirmation reply). |
| `/message <note>` | Sends the Superhuman a private note it works into its next turn. |
| `/urgent <note>` | Interrupts it immediately with your instruction. |
| Plain language | Steer it conversationally ("wrap up this topic", "you can jump in now"). |
| Questions from attendees | Answered in chat when chat Q&A is on. On Zoom, hosts can also DM it privately. |

**Real-time instructions from the console.** The live meeting screen has an
instruction box: **Act now** interrupts immediately; **Next turn** applies the next
time it speaks — all invisible to the other side.

**Live status, always visible.** The console shows where it is in its lifecycle:
Joining, In waiting room, Live, Rejoining, Host ended, Removed, Invalid link. If it
can't get in, the reason is specific.

**Leave, rejoin, remove.** Rejoin brings it back with full memory of the
conversation; Remove takes it off from the console (hosts can also mute/remove it
with native controls). Rejoining keeps everything under a single meeting record.

## The console: Upcoming, Live, and Meeting history

The Conferencing page is mission control, with your Superhuman's calendar email up
top and three tabs:

- **Upcoming** — Every scheduled meeting, who scheduled it, whether it's briefed, and a "Starting soon" badge within two minutes of start.
- **Live** — Meetings in flight, with live status, mode controls, real-time instructions, screen-share controls, documents, hosts, and the chat Q&A switch.
- **Meeting history** — Past meetings with duration, outcome, and an automatic summary; jump into Engagement Explorer for full review.

Plus role-aware visibility (members see their own meetings; admins see the
workspace's; hosts see meetings they co-host), filters by host and date, and honest
meeting outcomes (host-ended vs. removed vs. cancelled, with reasons for failed joins).

## Transparency: everyone knows it's an AI

- **It announces itself.** On join, a disclosure posts to the meeting chat (default "Superhuman AI has joined the meeting"; customizable per deployment and auto-translated).
- **It is visibly present.** It joins under a clear name you configure, camera on. There is no hidden listener mode.
- **It respects the room.** It waits in waiting rooms/lobbies until admitted, doesn't greet unprompted, and in its default mode doesn't speak at all.
- **Hosts stay in charge.** Hosts can mute or remove it with native controls; your team can pull it from the console anytime.
- **It only says and shows what you've approved.** It draws on the knowledge base your team configures, presents only materials it's been given, and follows guardrails for sensitive topics like pricing and competitive questions.
- **Only your team can send it in.** Inviting and controlling it requires an authorized user in your 1mind console.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| In waiting room | Waiting to be let in. Ask the host to admit it; it waits patiently and joins the moment it's admitted. |
| Invalid link | The link is malformed, expired, or points to a non-existent meeting. Double-check it; scheduling rejections also arrive by email with the reason. |
| Meeting locked | The host locked the meeting. Ask them to unlock it, then use Rejoin. |
| Sign-in or registration required | The meeting requires authenticated/registered participants. Loosen that for this meeting, or use a guest-allowed link. |
| Wrong password | The link's embedded passcode is missing or incorrect. Re-copy the full invite link including the passcode. |
| Not speaking when expected | Check the engagement mode: Silent never speaks; Standby waits for its wake word. Switch to Active, or type `/active` in chat. |
| No answers in the meeting chat | Chat Q&A may be off for this meeting or deployment. Flip the switch on the live meeting screen, or enable it when briefing. |

## Limits and fine print

- Session documents: up to 25 MB per file and 100 files per meeting.
- Meeting chat replies respect platform message limits; longer answers arrive as a short series of messages.
- Recurring series expand on daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly rules; sub-hourly recurrence and all-day events are not supported.
- Up to 50 overlapping scheduled meetings per workspace at any one time.
- Private chat (DMs) is available on Zoom; on Google Meet and Microsoft Teams the Superhuman uses the public meeting chat.
- Screen understanding follows one active presenter at a time.
- Multilingual calls are in beta and limited to the languages your persona is configured to speak and understand.

## Built for security review

- **Compliance posture** — 1mind maintains SOC 2 compliance, aligns with GDPR and CCPA, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and undergoes regular penetration testing. Details and reports are available through the [Trust Center](https://trust.1mind.com/).
- **Nothing to install** — The Superhuman joins as a standard participant. No plugins, extensions, marketplace apps, or OAuth grants are required for anyone in the meeting.
- **Authenticated media channels** — Real-time audio and screen streams flow over channels authenticated with per-session, expiring signed tokens. Event callbacks are signature-verified before they're trusted.
- **Data minimization** — Meeting audio and video are processed in real time to participate, not to build an archive. Individual participant video is never stored; post-call data handling is governed by your data agreement with 1mind.
