Sub-Agents extend what a Superhuman can do. Planned pages:

- **Live Demo** — reads the page, clicks, and narrates the demo
- **Turn-taking** — knowing when to speak, hold, or yield to a rep
- **Slide Generator** — net-new, on-brand slides in the moment
- **Presentation** — presenting content during a conversation
- **[Calendar & Routing](/using/sub-agents/calendar-routing)** — native scheduling and routing
- **Email Inbound** — follow-up on conversations that didn't book
- **Live Chat Handoff** — route to a human rep with full context
- **Ask-AI** — in-conversation, grounded answers

<Note>See [The five layers](/get-started/five-layers) for how Sub-Agents differ from Skills.</Note>
