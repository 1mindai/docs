Scheduling links let a rep or a team share a booking page outside a Superhuman conversation, for sequences and email signatures. The meetings dashboard shows what routing and those links produced.

## Personal links

Each rep has a personal link for each meeting type. A rep copies theirs from their profile, under **Scheduling**. A personal link follows the rep's own availability settings (working hours, time zone, buffers, and minimum notice).

## Team links

A team link books across the team through round-robin, in one of two modes:

- **Strict Round Robin**: the prospect is offered the next rep in the queue.
- **Flexible Round Robin**: the prospect is offered the team's combined availability, and the rep is assigned from who they pick.

Team-link bookings and Superhuman bookings share **one round-robin queue** per team, so a booking from either source affects the order for both. See [Manage a round-robin team](/configuring/integrations/round-robin-teams).

## The meetings dashboard

Admins read the dashboard to see what routing produced, without a Support data pull. It shows:

- Total, cancelled, and rescheduled meetings.
- Average meetings per rep.
- Meetings over time, with the change versus the prior period.
- A rep leaderboard, sorted by meetings booked.
- A team breakdown by meetings booked.
- A timeframe filter across all of the above.

### What the dashboard does not cover yet

- Meetings held versus no-shows.
- Pipeline or revenue attribution.
- Which page a meeting was booked from.
