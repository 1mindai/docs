## Capability statement

1mind's native Calendar & Routing lets prospects and customers book a meeting with
the right rep, at the right time, without leaving the Superhuman conversation. The
same Superhuman that qualifies a prospect resolves the correct owner from your CRM,
reads that rep's real-time calendar availability, presents open times inline, and
books the meeting — with the conversation summary automatically included in the
calendar invite. Routing follows your business rules (CRM account ownership, with
team round-robin and named-individual fallbacks).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Resolve the right rep">
    Looks up the account owner matched to the prospect in your CRM. If there's no
    match — or no availability in the configured window — routing advances to the
    fallback chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch real availability">
    Queries the resolved rep's live calendar (Google or Microsoft 365 / Outlook),
    respecting working hours, buffer time, minimum notice, and out-of-office.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present the scheduler">
    Surfaces available time slots to the prospect inline, with time zone auto-detected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the booking">
    Creates the calendar invite, attaches the virtual meeting link, embeds the
    conversation summary, and sends it to both parties — typically under 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Routing resolution order

| Priority | Source | Type | Availability window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CRM account owner | Individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 14) |
| 2 | Fallback 1 | Team (round-robin) or named individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 3) |
| 3+ | Additional fallbacks | Team (round-robin) or named individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 3) |

Out-of-office reps are automatically read as busy and excluded from round-robin.

## Specifications

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Calendar providers | Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 / Outlook (via OAuth) |
| Meeting-link auto-attach | Zoom, Google Meet (at launch) |
| Routing basis | CRM account ownership, with team round-robin and named-individual fallbacks |
| Availability windows | Configurable 1–14 days per step |
| Meeting types | Configurable durations: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 min |
| Time zone | Auto-detected for the prospect |
| Invite contents | Virtual meeting link + auto-included conversation summary |
| Reschedule / cancel | Via link in the confirmation email |
| Per-rep controls | Working hours, buffer time, minimum notice, same-day booking |
| Scheduling links | Personal (per rep × meeting type); team links with Strict or Flexible Round Robin |
| Tracking | Availability-shown and booking events captured in the transcript; exportable |

## Support matrix

| Scenario | Supported | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route by CRM account ownership | Yes | Owner matched via CRM lookup |
| Team round-robin fallback | Yes | Strict (next-in-queue) or Flexible (combined availability) |
| Named-individual fallback | Yes | Any number of ordered fallback steps |
| Real-time availability (Google, Microsoft 365 / Outlook) | Yes | Via OAuth; respects working hours, buffer, OOO |
| Conversation summary in invite | Yes | Auto-included on every booking |
| Reschedule / cancel | Yes | Link in confirmation email |
| Additional attendees + prospect context field | Yes | Optional |
| Microsoft Teams / Webex meeting links | Roadmap (Phase 2) | Zoom + Google Meet at launch |
| Advanced routing (account size, industry, custom fields) | Roadmap (Phase 2) | Launch routing is CRM-ownership-based |
| Group meetings with multiple reps on one invite | Not at launch | — |
| Customer-branded scheduling UI | Not at launch | Uses 1mind defaults; embedded in your Superhuman |
| Multiple calendar providers within one org | Not at launch | — |
| Self-serve routing configuration | Not at launch | Configured by the 1mind Services team |

## Configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Workspace & rep setup (admin + each rep)">
    Admin invites reps; each rep connects a calendar (Google or Microsoft 365 /
    Outlook) and meeting provider, and sets working hours and time zone. Reps
    without both connected cannot receive bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meeting types, teams & routing logic (admin / RevOps)">
    Admin creates meeting types and teams (e.g., by region or segment), then
    documents routing rules and fallbacks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation & validation (1mind Services)">
    Services implements routing and coordinates validation. Customer self-serve
    routing configuration is planned but not available at launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits & constraints

- Routing workflows are configured by the 1mind Services team at launch.
- One calendar provider per org at launch.
- No group meetings with multiple reps on a single invite at launch.
- Scheduler uses 1mind default styling at launch.
