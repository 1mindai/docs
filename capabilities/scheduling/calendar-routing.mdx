## Capability statement

1mind's native Calendar Routing & Scheduling lets prospects and customers book a
meeting with the right rep, at the right time, without leaving the Superhuman
conversation. The same Superhuman that qualifies a prospect resolves the correct
owner from your CRM, reads that rep's real-time calendar availability, presents
open times inline, and books the meeting — with the conversation summary
automatically included in the calendar invite. Routing follows your business
rules (CRM account ownership, with team round-robin and named-individual
fallbacks), so inbound leads are booked in one flow rather than handed off to a
separate scheduling tool.

## How it works

When a prospect signals meeting intent, the Superhuman runs a routing and
booking sequence in a single step:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Resolve the right rep">
    Looks up the account owner matched to the prospect in your CRM. If there is
    no owner match — or the owner has no availability in the configured window —
    routing advances to the fallback chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch real availability">
    Queries the resolved rep's live calendar (Google or Microsoft 365 / Outlook),
    respecting working hours, buffer time, minimum notice, and out-of-office.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present the scheduler">
    Surfaces available time slots to the prospect inline in the conversation,
    with the prospect's time zone auto-detected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the booking">
    Creates the calendar invite, attaches the virtual meeting link, embeds the
    conversation summary, and sends it to both parties — typically in under 30
    seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Routing resolution order

Availability is resolved top-to-bottom; the first step that returns open slots is
shown to the prospect.

| Priority | Source | Type | Availability window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CRM account owner | Individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 14) |
| 2 | Fallback 1 | Team (round-robin) or named individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 3) |
| 3+ | Additional fallbacks | Team (round-robin) or named individual | Configurable 1–14 days (default 3) |

If every step is exhausted with no availability, the workflow's terminal
fallback behavior applies. Out-of-office reps are automatically read as busy and
excluded from round-robin for that period, with no admin action required.

## Configuration

Setup spans three areas with distinct owners (see the Services Onboarding Guide
for the full walkthrough):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Workspace & rep setup (admin + each rep)">
    Admin invites reps in **Settings → User Management**. Each rep connects their
    calendar (Google or Microsoft 365 / Outlook) and meeting provider, and sets
    working hours and time zone. Reps without a connected calendar and meeting
    provider cannot receive bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meeting types, teams & routing logic (admin / RevOps)">
    Admin creates meeting types (title, duration, description) and teams (e.g., by
    region or segment) that power round-robin distribution, then documents the
    routing rules and fallbacks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation & validation (1mind Services)">
    The 1mind Services team implements the routing logic and coordinates
    validation. Customer self-serve routing configuration is planned but not
    available at launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits & constraints

- Routing workflows are configured by the 1mind Services team; customer
  self-serve configuration is not available at launch.
- One calendar provider per customer org at launch.
- No group meetings with multiple reps on a single invite at launch.
- Scheduler uses 1mind default styling at launch (no custom branding).
- Reps must connect both a calendar and a meeting provider before they can
  receive bookings.
