## What knowledge integrations do

Knowledge integrations let a Superhuman ground its answers in your own content, 
product documentation, sales decks, case studies, help-center articles, and more.
You connect a content source once; 1mind syncs the files, converts them into
knowledge the Superhuman can retrieve, and keeps them current on a schedule you set.

## Available connectors

The following source connectors are available today:

- **Google Drive**
- **Microsoft SharePoint**
- **Microsoft OneDrive**
- **Box**
- **Dropbox**
- **Confluence** (Cloud only)
- **Highspot**
- **Salesforce** (Knowledge articles)
- **Notion**
- **Seismic**
- **SFTP**

In development: **Jira**.

<Note>Connector availability changes over time, check the console for the current list, or ask your 1mind CSM.</Note>

## How it works

1. A Workspace Admin or Super Admin adds a connector and authenticates to the source.
2. You select the folders, sites, spaces, or libraries to sync.
3. 1mind syncs the content on your chosen frequency (every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours).
4. Synced files land as **drafts** in your Sandbox. You review and **publish** them to make them live to the Superhuman.
5. Ongoing changes flow through automatically, edits re-sync, and deletes at the source remove the corresponding knowledge.

## Key facts to know before you start

- **Permissions:** Only **Workspace Admins** and **Super Admins** can add or manage connectors.
- **One connector per type:** You can connect one instance of each source type (for example, one Google Drive connection).
- **Draft-then-publish:** Newly synced files are drafts in Sandbox until you publish them, nothing goes live automatically.
- **Deletes propagate:** Removing a file at the source removes it from 1mind on the next sync.
- **File size:** The per-file limit is **100MB**; we recommend keeping files **under 50MB** for reliable processing.
- **Sync frequency:** Choose 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours per connector.

## Removing synced content

You can remove synced content from 1mind yourself in the Knowledge Hub. You do not need to open a support request, and removing content in 1mind never changes the files in your source. 1mind connects with read access only.

For the file connectors (Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Box, Dropbox), you have three options:

- **Remove specific documents, keep your source files.** Delete the documents in the Knowledge Hub. Your source files are untouched, a deleted document stays deleted even if the source file is edited later, and a later sync of the same connection does not bring it back.
- **Stop syncing new changes, keep what is already in.** Disconnect the source. Disconnecting stops future syncs and leaves the documents already ingested in place.
- **Remove a document everywhere by removing the original.** Deleting the file at the source removes it from 1mind on the next sync. This is the only option that also loses your original, so use it last.

To remove a source from one Superhuman without deleting it from the workspace, disable it for that Superhuman instead. See [Superhuman Knowledge](/configuring/knowledge/superhuman-knowledge) and the destructive actions in the [Knowledge overview](/configuring/knowledge/overview).
