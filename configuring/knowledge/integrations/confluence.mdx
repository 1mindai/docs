## What you'll need

- **Workspace Admin** or **Super Admin** access in the 1mind Console.
- **Confluence Cloud** (Confluence Server / Data Center is not supported).
- Your Confluence **username (email)**, **site domain**, and an **API token**.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Confluence API token">
    In your Atlassian account, generate an **API token** for the user that will
    authorize the connection.
    {/* screenshot: Atlassian API token page */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Confluence connector in 1mind">
    In the Console, open **Knowledge → Integrations** and choose **Confluence**.
    {/* screenshot: connector gallery */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Confluence details">
    Provide your **username (email)**, Confluence **site domain**, and the **API
    token**, then authenticate.
    {/* screenshot: Confluence credential entry */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select spaces to sync">
    Choose the Confluence spaces or pages to include.
    {/* screenshot: space picker */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sync frequency and publish">
    Pick every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours. Synced pages arrive as drafts in your
    Sandbox — review and publish to go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Cloud only** — Confluence Server / Data Center is not supported.
- One Confluence connection per workspace.
- 100MB per-file limit; keep files under 50MB for reliable processing.
- Edits and deletes at the source flow through on the next sync.
