## What you'll need

- **Workspace Admin** or **Super Admin** access in the 1mind Console.
- A Notion account with access to the pages you want to sync, and permission to authorize an integration.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Notion connector">
    In the Console, open **Knowledge → Integrations** and choose **Notion**.
    {/* screenshot: connector gallery */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Notion">
    Authenticate with Notion (OAuth) and grant 1mind read access to the pages you
    select during authorization.
    {/* screenshot: Notion OAuth consent + page selection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select pages to sync">
    Choose the Notion pages to include. Notion access is scoped to the pages you
    share with the integration.
    {/* screenshot: page picker */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sync frequency">
    Pick every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts and publish">
    Synced pages arrive as drafts in your Sandbox — review and publish to go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- One Notion connection per workspace.
- The Superhuman can only see the pages you share with the integration.
- Edits and deletes at the source flow through on the next sync.
