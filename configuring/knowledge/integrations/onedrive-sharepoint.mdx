## What you'll need

- **Workspace Admin** or **Super Admin** access in the 1mind Console.
- A Microsoft 365 account that can access the OneDrive folders or SharePoint sites you want to sync.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In the Console, open **Knowledge → Integrations** and choose **OneDrive** or
    **SharePoint**.
    {/* screenshot: connector gallery */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Microsoft">
    Authenticate with Microsoft (OAuth) and grant 1mind read access.
    {/* screenshot: Microsoft OAuth consent */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select what to sync">
    For OneDrive, choose folders. For SharePoint, choose the sites, libraries, or
    folders to include.
    {/* screenshot: site/folder picker */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sync frequency">
    Pick every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review drafts and publish">
    Synced files arrive as drafts in your Sandbox — review and publish to go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- One OneDrive and one SharePoint connection per workspace.
- 100MB per-file limit; keep files under 50MB for reliable processing.
- Edits and deletes at the source flow through on the next sync.
