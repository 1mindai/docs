## What you'll need

- **Workspace Admin** or **Super Admin** access in the 1mind Console.
- A Salesforce org with **Knowledge** articles, and permission to create a connected app.
- From Salesforce: your **Instance URL**, **Consumer Key**, and **Consumer Secret** (from an External Client App / connected app).

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an External Client App in Salesforce">
    In Salesforce, create an External Client App (connected app) to authorize 1mind.
    Note the **Consumer Key** and **Consumer Secret** it generates, and your org's
    **Instance URL**.
    {/* screenshot: Salesforce External Client App config */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Salesforce connector in 1mind">
    In the Console, open **Knowledge → Integrations** and choose **Salesforce**.
    {/* screenshot: connector gallery */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Salesforce credentials">
    Provide the **Instance URL**, **Consumer Key**, and **Consumer Secret**, then
    authenticate.
    {/* screenshot: Salesforce credential entry */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sync">
    Select the Knowledge articles to include. Use the **Presentable documents** toggle
    to control which documents are made available to the Superhuman.
    {/* screenshot: article selection + Presentable toggle */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set sync frequency and publish">
    Pick every 1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 hours. Synced articles arrive as drafts in your
    Sandbox — review and publish to go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Syncs Salesforce **Knowledge articles** (not general CRM records).
- One Salesforce Knowledge connection per workspace.
- 100MB per-file limit; keep files under 50MB for reliable processing.
- Edits and deletes at the source flow through on the next sync.
