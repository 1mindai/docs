Website crawling brings your site's content — your primary domain, subdomains, and
their pages — into the 1mind **Knowledge Hub**. 1mind crawls the site to discover
every URL, scrapes the content from each page, and feeds it into the Superhuman's
Brain. On a refresh schedule, the Hub re-crawls to keep content current — no manual
export or re-upload.

## Key concepts

- **Domain vs. subdomain.** The **domain** is the root site (`hubspot.com`). A **subdomain** is a separate section under its own prefix (`knowledge.hubspot.com`). Crawling the domain doesn't automatically capture subdomains — each is crawled independently (the "Include subdomains" option handles this). Individual page URLs (`hubspot.com/products`) are picked up automatically.
- **Crawl vs. scrape.** *Crawl* discovers all the URLs on a site; *scrape* extracts the actual content from each. 1mind does both in one step.

## What we crawl by default

For most customers, the standard approach is:

- **Crawl:** the full primary domain plus help / support / knowledge subdomains.
- **Exclude:** blogs, news & press, events & webinars, legal / privacy / terms, and career pages.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Auto-refresh:** monthly (weekly on request).

## What we need from you

To set up your crawl, provide:

- **Primary domain** (e.g., `acme.com`).
- **Subdomains to include** (or confirm the default of including help/support/knowledge subdomains).
- **Exclusions** beyond the defaults — any paths you don't want crawled (e.g., `/careers`).
- **Language(s)** — English by default; tell us if you need others.
- **Refresh frequency** — monthly by default, or weekly.

<Note>
  Share any custom exclusions **before** the crawl starts. Removing URLs after the
  fact takes significantly longer than excluding them up front.
</Note>

## Gated content and files

Gated or login-only content can't be crawled. For that material, either un-gate the
pages and share the URLs, or provide the files (PDFs, docs) to your 1mind Google
Drive folder so they can be added to the Knowledge Hub directly.

## Keeping content current

The Hub re-crawls on your refresh schedule (monthly by default), so ongoing site
changes are picked up automatically. If you relaunch or substantially update your
site, let your 1mind contact know so the source can be re-ingested — this creates a
new version while preserving the prior one.

## Optional: page images

If your content relies on images, diagrams, or screenshots (common for CS or
developer content), ask to enable image capture so those display in the
conversation. It's off by default and best left off for inbound marketing sites.
