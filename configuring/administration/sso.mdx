## Overview

This guide helps 1mind customers configure SAML 2.0 Single Sign-On (SSO) with
Identity Providers such as Okta, Azure AD (Microsoft Entra ID), or Google
Workspace. SSO lets your team authenticate through your organization's existing
Identity Provider (IdP), providing centralized access control, enhanced security,
and simplified user management.

## Prerequisites

**What the 1mind team completes first** (before sending your invitation):

- Creates your organization's account
- Basic initial configuration of your 1mind Superhuman
- Sends the invitation to your Genesis User (your first 1mind Console admin)
- Enables the SSO feature

**What you need (customer):**

- Administrative access to your Identity Provider (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, etc.)
- Ability to create and configure enterprise applications in your IdP
- The invitation email from 1mind (sent after account setup is complete)

<Note>
  **What is a Genesis User?** The first administrator who configures the initial
  SSO setup. They are the only user who signs in for the first time with a
  password. If you're an existing 1mind customer with password-credentialed users,
  those users will be prompted to sign in via SSO once an admin configures it.
</Note>

## Setup process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initial account setup">
    The Genesis User signs up with **email** and **password** before SSO can be
    configured: open the invitation email, click the signup link, create your
    account (first name, last name, password), and verify your email with the
    activation code. You'll land on the 1mind Console.

    <Warning>The password you create is your fallback login if SSO has issues. Keep it secure.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access SSO configuration">
    In the 1mind Console, go to **Settings** (left sidebar) → **SSO** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email domain">
    Click **Configure SSO**, enter your organization's **domain only** (correct:
    `company.com`; incorrect: `user@company.com`), then **Generate Onboarding
    Link**. You'll get a configuration link (valid for 1 hour); click **Log out &
    open link** to reach the configuration portal, then **Configure Single
    Sign-On**.
    {/* screenshot: onboarding link modal + configuration portal */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your Identity Provider">
    Choose your IdP — Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), Okta, Google, or Generic SAML
    IdP — and follow the wizard, which guides you through creating an application,
    configuring Service Provider (SP) values in your IdP, setting up the SAML
    connection, and attribute mapping.
    {/* screenshot: IdP options + Entra ID wizard */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your SSO configuration">
    Click **Test sign-in** in the last step of the wizard. You'll be redirected to
    the 1mind sign-in page; enter your company email, choose **Sign in with
    [your IdP]**, and authenticate. As the Genesis User, you'll be prompted once to
    enter your existing 1mind password to link your SSO credentials to your account
    — this is only required once; all subsequent logins use your IdP.
    {/* screenshot: test sign-in + credential-merge prompt */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite users to 1mind">
    In the Console, go to **Settings → User Management → Add User**, enter each
    teammate's work email (the one they use with your IdP), and send. They click
    the invitation link to sign in via SSO for the first time.

    <Note>Existing password-credentialed users are prompted to set their password once as part of a credential merge, like the Genesis User.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The configuration link expired. How do I get a new one?">
    Configuration links are valid for 1 hour. In the Console go to Settings → SSO → Configure SSO to generate a new link, and complete setup within the time limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see the SSO tab in Settings. Why not?">
    Either SSO isn't enabled for your account, or you don't have Admin permissions. Contact your 1mind CSM or Services contact to enable the feature.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SSO was working but now it's not. What happened?">
    The most common cause is an expired IdP certificate. Check your Identity Provider's certificate expiration date and upload a new one if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if SSO login fails?">
    Confirm the ACS URL and SP Entity ID in your IdP match exactly what's shown in the 1mind wizard (watch for extra spaces/typos), and verify the IdP metadata (certificate, SSO URL, Issuer) matches your current configuration. If it persists, contact your 1mind representative with screenshots and details of when the failure occurs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Users can sign in through SSO but get access denied / unauthorized">
    They authenticated with your IdP, but their account isn't assigned to 1mind yet. An IT admin needs to assign them to the 1mind application in your IdP (Okta/Azure AD/Google Workspace). Ask IT to add the users to the 1mind application group.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
