For a Superhuman to book meetings, each rep connects their calendar and a meeting provider to 1mind. 1mind then reads their **real-time availability** and books on their actual calendar, attaching a virtual meeting link automatically. This page covers the connection; for how availability and routing surface inside the conversation, see [Calendar & Routing](/using/sub-agents/calendar-routing).

## Prerequisites

- A 1mind Superhuman created and configured for your workspace.
- Each rep who should receive bookings is invited to the Console (Settings → User Management).

## Connect a calendar and meeting provider

Each rep completes this in their own account (about 5 minutes):

1. Sign in to the Console at `app.1mind.com`.
2. Open the **profile icon** (top right) → **My Profile** → **Scheduling & Meeting** settings.
3. **Connect your calendar.** Supported providers: **Microsoft Outlook** and **Google** (real-time availability via OAuth).
4. **Connect your meeting provider.** Supported providers: **Microsoft Teams**, **Google Meet**, and **Zoom**.

<Note>If you connect Microsoft or Google as your calendar, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet (respectively) is already connected as your meeting provider — no extra step unless you prefer Zoom.</Note>

## Set availability preferences

On the same Scheduling & Meeting page, each rep sets their **working hours** and confirms their **working time zone**. Routing combines these preferences with each rep's live calendar availability, so they need to be accurate. Out-of-office blocks are read as busy automatically.

## Frequently asked questions

- **If a rep isn't connected, can prospects still book with them?** No. A rep must have a connected calendar and meeting provider before they can receive bookings — 1mind won't send an invite without a virtual meeting link.
- **Are booked meetings tracked in the Console?** Yes. When availability is presented or a meeting is booked, the event is captured in the conversation transcript, which can also be exported.
