For a Superhuman to book meetings, each rep connects their own calendar and a meeting provider to 1mind. 1mind then reads their **real-time availability** and books on their actual calendar, attaching a virtual meeting link automatically. This page is one a CSM can send to a rep: it covers connecting, setting availability, and reconnecting if the connection drops. For how availability and routing surface inside the conversation, see [Calendar & Routing](/using/sub-agents/calendar-routing).

<Warning>
A rep needs **both** a connected calendar and a connected meeting provider to receive meetings. A rep missing either one is skipped by routing, and nothing warns them. Connect both, then confirm availability.
</Warning>

## Prerequisites

- A 1mind Superhuman created and configured for your workspace.
- Each rep who should receive bookings is invited to the Console (Settings → User Management). Inviting a rep does not connect their calendar; each rep connects their own.

## Connect a calendar and meeting provider

Each rep completes this in their own account (about 5 minutes). An admin cannot connect a calendar for someone else.

1. Sign in to the Console at `app.1mind.com`.
2. Open the **profile icon** (top right) → **My Profile** → **Scheduling & Meeting** settings.
3. **Connect your calendar.** Supported providers: **Microsoft Outlook** and **Google** (real-time availability via secure sign-in).
4. **Connect your meeting provider.** Supported providers: **Microsoft Teams**, **Google Meet**, and **Zoom**.

<Note>If you connect Microsoft or Google as your calendar, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet (respectively) is already connected as your meeting provider, with no extra step unless you prefer Zoom.</Note>

Each rep authorizes their own account, so reps in the same workspace can use different providers. One rep on Google and another on Outlook with Zoom is fully supported.

## Set your availability

On the same **Scheduling & Meeting** page, each rep sets the preferences routing uses alongside their live calendar. Keep them accurate:

- **Working hours** on each working day.
- **Working time zone.**
- **Minimum notice** before a meeting can be booked.
- **Buffers** before and after meetings.
- **Date overrides** for days your hours differ from the usual week.

Out-of-office blocks on your own calendar are read as busy automatically, so routing will not book over them.

## Reconnect a lost connection

A connection can drop when access is revoked, a company security change removes it, or a device is reset. When that happens, routing stops booking for that rep until it is reconnected. The rep reconnects it themselves:

1. Open **My Profile → Scheduling & Meeting**.
2. Reconnect the calendar and meeting provider, signing in again when prompted.
3. Confirm the connection shows as active.

An admin cannot reconnect on a rep's behalf.

## If your company uses Microsoft

Some Microsoft tenants require a one-time admin approval before reps can connect Outlook. The first rep to connect sends an approval request to the company's Microsoft admin, and every rep's connection waits on it. This is a Microsoft setting, not an SSO problem.

## Frequently asked questions

- **If a rep isn't connected, can prospects still book with them?** No. A rep needs a connected calendar and meeting provider before they can receive bookings; 1mind will not send an invite without a virtual meeting link.
- **Can an admin connect calendars for the whole team at once?** No. Connections are made per rep. An admin can confirm who has connected but cannot authorize on someone else's behalf.
- **Are booked meetings tracked in the Console?** Yes. When availability is presented or a meeting is booked, the event is captured in the conversation transcript, which can be exported.
