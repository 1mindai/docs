## Overview

With 1mind's Superhumans + Chili Piper, a website visitor can talk, qualify, and
book a meeting in a single flow — accelerating pipeline, routing intelligently on
CRM/MAP data, and handing off seamlessly from digital conversation to a human rep.

**Who does what:**

- **1mind** hosts the Superhuman on your site, generates unique Smart Booking Links, and passes CRM/MAP/account data into Chili Piper for routing.
- **You (customer)** configure routing/assignment in Chili Piper (queues, owner rules, round-robin, territory), provide your router/queue URL and required parameters, and keep your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics) integration active for booking + activity sync.
- **Chili Piper** applies your queue/owner rules from the identifiers provided (primarily email), presents availability, books the meeting, and updates the CRM.

## How the flow works

1. A visitor engages with a Superhuman on your website.
2. The Superhuman runs lightweight qualification (name, email, company, region, use case).
3. Based on the conversation, 1mind constructs your Chili Piper router/queue link with the right identifiers.
4. The visitor clicks the link → Chili Piper routes to the correct rep/team and books directly.
5. Chili Piper logs the meeting back into your CRM.

## Link construction

**Base router/queue link (you provide):** `https://calendar.chilipiper.com/router/inbound-bdr` *(example — use your actual router/queue URL).*

**Minimum parameter (required):**

- `id=<prospect_email>` — the primary identifier Chili Piper uses to match/create the record and apply routing.

**Common optional parameters:**

- `meetingTypeId=<UUID>` — selects a specific meeting template/flow (e.g., Demo, Discovery). *Legacy workspaces may use `type=<name>` instead.*
- `by=<name-or-id>` — credits the booker (e.g., SDR→AE handoff reporting).

**Example (BDR demo):**

```text
https://calendar.chilipiper.com/router/inbound-bdr?id=john.smith@acme.com&meetingTypeId=<your_meeting_type_uuid>&by=superhuman
```

<Note>In a prospecting flow we typically don't rely on Account or Opportunity IDs gathered in-chat — Chili Piper routes correctly from the email plus your configured rules.</Note>

## What the Superhuman collects

First & last name (rep context), email (required for routing/CRM match), company
name, country/region (for territory rules), and use case/request type (can inform
`meetingTypeId`). These are gathered conversationally and can be echoed to the rep
via the calendar description or CRM notes.

## Updating Data Fields

The values the Superhuman collects must match the values in Chili Piper's **Data
Fields** page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Data Fields">Navigate to **Data Fields** in your workspace.</Step>
  <Step title="Add Smart Parameters">
    For each field being used (e.g., First Name), add a value under **Smart
    Parameter** — e.g., `firstName`. Continue for all collected fields
    (`lastName`, `email`, `company`, …), using camelCase where applicable.
    {/* screenshot: Data Fields Smart Parameter config */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Under **Meeting Type**, enabling **"Skip the Form when possible"** lets the user bypass the form and just pick a time when the Superhuman has passed all fields. If disabled, the form is shown pre-filled for the user to edit before booking.</Note>

## Routing patterns (configured in Chili Piper)

- **Owner-based** (email matches Lead/Contact/Account owner)
- **Round-robin** across a team/queue
- **Territory-based** queues (country/region/state)
- **Meeting-type** queues (Demo vs. Discovery vs. Onboarding)

1mind doesn't change your routing logic — we pass the right identifiers so your
Chili Piper configuration applies cleanly.

## Security & data handling

1mind collects only the minimal fields needed for scheduling; links are generated
per interaction and include only approved parameters; meeting outcomes and CRM
writes occur within your Chili Piper + CRM connection.
