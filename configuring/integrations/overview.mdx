1mind connects to the systems you already run so your Superhumans can identify visitors, book meetings, sync data, and stay in sync with your stack. Integrations are configured in the Console under **Integrations** (workspace scope). This page orients you to what's available and links to the setup guide for each.

## CRM & marketing automation

Sync qualified conversations, contacts, and outcomes to your systems of record.

- [Salesforce integration](/configuring/integrations/salesforce/overview) and [Salesforce OAuth & package install](/configuring/integrations/salesforce/oauth-setup)
- [Marketo integration](/configuring/integrations/marketo)
- [CRM / MAP & Web Visitor ID setup](/configuring/integrations/crm-map-setup) — what 1mind needs from you to connect and map fields

## Scheduling & routing
