## Introduction

The 1mind Superhuman acts as an always-on online BDR and Sales Engineer
(MEDDPICC / BANT / SPICED). This guide details its integration with **Marketo**:
using your Person data and activity history, it personalizes engagement, identifies
high-intent leads, and routes them to sales — or adds them to nurture — with
summarized and detailed insights.

## Integration approach

- **Intelligent pre-processing (read):** access Person data and summarized activity so conversations are informed from the first interaction, optimized for speed.
- **Actionable post-processing (write):** update Marketo records with qualification details, engagement signals, conversation summaries, and full transcript.
- **Accelerated workflow enhancement:** integrate with your existing programs and sales processes — route qualified leads to sales or into Smart Campaigns for nurture.

### What pre-processing reads

- **Person/Lead:** `GET /rest/v1/lead/{id}.json`, `GET /rest/v1/leads.json` (batch by email/filter). Fields: name, email, job title, company, industry, lead score, acquisition program, and custom scoring fields.
- **Activity insights (summarized):** use custom aggregated fields (`Recent_Web_Activity__c`, `Aggregated_Email_Engagement_Score__c`, `Last_Content_Topic__c`, `Recent_Program_Memberships__c`, `Intent_Activity_Score__c`) populated via Smart Campaigns, batch workflows, or Data Value Change triggers — instead of querying raw activity records.
- **Program membership:** `GET /rest/v1/leads/{leadId}/programs.json` to detect content engagement, event attendance, and webinar registrations.

### What post-processing writes

- 1mind-derived insights, qualification metadata, conversation summaries, full transcript
- API: `POST /rest/v1/leads.json` with `action=createOrUpdate`, including custom fields
- Optional: custom objects, full conversation URLs, structured summaries

## Part 2: Technical integration

### Access & authentication

Recommended: a **Marketo Custom Service with OAuth 2.0 (Client Credentials
Grant)** — created in **Admin → LaunchPoint**, token endpoint
`/identity/oauth/token` with `client_id` and `client_secret`. Least-privilege
scope:

- **Read (pre-processing):** Read Activities, Read Leads, Read Programs
- **Write (post-processing):** Create/Update Leads, Create/Update Custom Objects

### Data flow

**Pre-processing — user identification (critical):** your web infrastructure must
pass `marketoContactId` or `email` (from cookie, local storage, or web session) to
the launcher — these are inputs to the Marketo Lead API. Without it, the Superhuman
engages anonymously. **Fallback:** if a third-party vendor (ZoomInfo, 6sense)
de-anonymizes the visitor and email is available, 1mind attempts Get Lead by Email.
Retrieval focuses on minimal, recent data (last 1–2 email clicks, recent page
visits, company/title/leadScore) for a fast payload; data is injected into the
session in real time.

**Post-processing — Person custom fields:** `1mind_Qualification_Status__c`,
`1mind_Budget_Identified__c`, `1mind_Authority_Identified__c`,
`1mind_Need_Pain_Points__c`, `1mind_Timeline_Identified__c`,
`1mind_Impact_Discussed__c`, `1mind_Decision_Criteria__c`,
`1mind_Champion_Identified__c`, `1mind_Competition_Discussed__c`,
`1mind_Product_Interest__c`, `1mind_Next_Steps_Proposed__c`,
`1mind_Engagement_Duration_Seconds__c`, `1mind_Conversation_Topics__c`,
`1mind_Explicit_Opt_in_Preferences__c`.

**Custom object (`1mind_Superhuman_Interaction__c`)** for richer derived insight:
`Derived_Urgency_Indicator__c`, `Derived_Key_Themes_Discussed__c`,
`Derived_Specific_Objections_Raised__c`, `Derived_Competitive_Mentions__c`,
`Derived_Ideal_Customer_Profile_Fit__c`, `Derived_Opportunity_Expansion_Signals__c`,
`Derived_Follow_Up_Call_Guidance__c`, `Derived_Resources_Presented_Detail__c`,
`Derived_Action_Proposed_By_Agent__c`, plus `1mind_Summary__c`,
`1mind_Top_Topics__c`, and `Full_Transcript__c` (long text, ~30K min).

### Objects & fields

- **Objects:** Person (core), Activity (history), Program (attribution), optional Custom Object (derived insight).
- **Read:** `id, firstName, lastName, email, company, title, industry, leadScore, lastActivityDate`, CRM sync IDs, and recent activities (page visits, email clicks, form fillouts, content downloads).
- **Write:** the `1mind_` Person fields above and the custom-object fields.

### Workflow, reporting, and environment

- **Workflow:** setting `1mind_Qualification_Status__c = "1QL"` (or a high-intent derived field) triggers your existing Marketo Flow Steps — owner assignment (synced to CRM), AE/BDR task creation, and alert emails. Relies on your Marketo–CRM sync to push `1mind_` fields to the CRM.
- **Reporting:** track `1mind_Qualification_Status__c` conversions and lead-score changes in Marketo; conversion-to-Opportunity in CRM; and raw 1mind data (JSON/CSV to S3/warehouse) for bespoke dashboards (engagements, conversion rates, avg engagement time, top topics, objections, competitive mentions, BDR/SDR time saved).
- **Environment:** request Sandbox/Production access, define testing scenarios (known vs. anonymous, qualified vs. unqualified), phased deployment, and collaborate with Marketo/Web/CRM/Data Engineering teams.
