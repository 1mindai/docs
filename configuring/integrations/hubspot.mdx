## Inbound Website SDR

The 1mind HubSpot integration connects your Inbound Website SDR to HubSpot at three points in every conversation: before it starts, while it's happening, and after it ends. Your sellers see what the Superhuman learned directly on the Contact record, and your HubSpot workflows can act on it.

<Info>
  1mind Services builds each integration with your team, based on the data you want captured, your business rules, and how your HubSpot account is configured.
</Info>

### Requirements

- A HubSpot **Super Admin**, or a user who can install apps, create properties, and (if used) create custom objects.
- Admin access to your 1mind workspace.
- A HubSpot **sandbox** account (strongly recommended). 1mind connects to your sandbox first, then to production once testing is complete.
- Your **web development team**, to place the Superhuman on your site.

<Note>
  The optional 1mind Conversation custom object requires a **HubSpot Enterprise** subscription with available custom-object capacity.
</Note>

#### Permissions

The connection requests the following HubSpot scopes:

| Scope | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oauth` | Yes | Standard HubSpot app connection |
| `crm.objects.contacts.read` | Yes | Recognize known visitors and personalize the conversation |
| `crm.objects.contacts.write` | Yes | Write conversation outcomes to the Contact |
| `crm.objects.companies.read` | Optional | Required only if Company (account) details are brought into the conversation |
| `crm.objects.custom.read` | Optional | Required only for the 1mind Conversation custom object. Prevents duplicate records on retry. |
| `crm.objects.custom.write` | Optional | Required only for the 1mind Conversation custom object. Creates records and associates them to Contacts. |
| `forms` | Optional | Required only if the Superhuman submits HubSpot forms during the conversation |

### How the integration works

| Phase | When | What 1mind does with HubSpot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Before the conversation** | The visitor has landed on your site but hasn't started talking | Captures the web session (HubSpot cookie, UTMs, URL values, form values) and looks up the visitor in HubSpot |
| **During the conversation** | The visitor and the Superhuman are talking | Looks up visitors who identify themselves, submits HubSpot forms, and shows HubSpot Meetings links |
| **After the conversation** | The conversation has ended | Writes conversation data back to HubSpot: the Contact, the optional 1mind Conversation record, and any custom fields you choose |

### Deployment types

Where the Superhuman lives on your site determines what it knows at the start of a conversation. Integration work carries over between deployment types, so many teams deploy more than one.

- **Widget:** Floats in the corner of your pages as the visitor scrolls. You choose which pages it appears on.
- **Embedded:** Lives on a dedicated landing page. Well suited to campaigns that drive traffic to a single page.
- **Behind the form:** Appears after a visitor submits one of your forms, typically on the thank-you page. The submitted values are passed to the Superhuman, so the conversation starts with context about who the visitor is.

### Before the conversation

#### What 1mind can capture

- **HubSpot tracking cookie (`hubspotutk`):** If a visitor has been cookied by HubSpot, for example by submitting a form or clicking a marketing email, 1mind can look up their Contact and read the fields you approve.
- **UTMs and URL values:** Campaign, source, medium, and other values in the page URL. These are written back after the conversation for attribution.
- **Other session values:** Anything else your site stores in the session, such as first-party cookies or data from de-anonymization tools like 6sense.
- **Form values (behind-the-form only):** The fields the visitor just submitted.

#### Deciding what the Superhuman says

Knowing something about a visitor doesn't mean the Superhuman should mention it. For example, if the HubSpot cookie returns a first name, should the Superhuman greet the visitor by name before they've introduced themselves?

You make this decision **field by field**. For each value, choose whether the Superhuman can reference it in conversation, or use it only in the background and write it back to HubSpot afterward.

<Tip>
  Start conservative and expand over time as you see how visitors respond.
</Tip>

#### How 1mind treats identity

- A visitor is treated as a known Contact when 1mind has a trusted identifier: a HubSpot cookie match, a HubSpot Contact ID passed by your site, or an email address passed by your site or provided during the conversation.
- 1mind assigns each visitor a 1mind visitor ID, which can be written to HubSpot to link repeat conversations. The visitor ID is never used on its own to identify a person.
- Without a trusted identifier, the conversation starts anonymously.

### During the conversation

While the visitor is talking with the Superhuman, 1mind can interact with HubSpot in three ways.

#### Identifying visitors mid-conversation

If 1mind couldn't identify the visitor from the HubSpot cookie before the conversation, it can try again once the visitor shares their email address. 1mind searches HubSpot for a matching Contact and, if one exists, brings the approved Contact details and associated Company (account) information into the Superhuman's context.

The Superhuman can then tailor the rest of the conversation, following the same field-by-field rules you set in [Deciding what the Superhuman says](#deciding-what-the-superhuman-says). If no matching Contact is found, the conversation continues and the visitor's details are written to HubSpot after the conversation, based on your create and overwrite rules.

#### Submitting HubSpot forms

The Superhuman can display one of your HubSpot forms in the conversation, pre-filled with what it already knows (if the form supports reading from URL parameters). The visitor reviews and submits it, and the submission flows through your existing HubSpot form workflows.  1mind can also submit the form via API by posting to the Hubspot Forms API after the conversation ends. 1mind will need to Form GUID and Hubspot Portal Id. This requires the optional `forms` scope and RECAPTCHA turned off on that Form.

#### Booking with HubSpot Meetings

If you use HubSpot Meetings, the Superhuman can show the right meeting link in the conversation in one of two ways:

- **HubSpot routing:** The Superhuman pre-fills your HubSpot routing form if your form accepts reading from URL parameters, and HubSpot sends the visitor to the correct rep's meeting link, as it does today.
- **1mind routing:** 1mind Services configures your routing rules in the Superhuman, which shows the correct HubSpot Meetings link directly.

Meetings booked through HubSpot Meetings appear in HubSpot as usual. For other calendaring tools and routing options, see [Calendaring](/path-to-calendaring-guide).

### After the conversation

When the conversation ends, 1mind writes what it captured in the conversation back to HubSpot. You decide where that data lives, which fields to capture, and how existing data is handled.

#### Where conversation data lives

| Option | Benefits | Considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contact properties** | Simple to set up, easy for sellers to find, available on any HubSpot plan | Holds only the most recent conversation. Each new conversation replaces the previous values. |
| **1mind Conversation custom object** | One record per conversation, preserving full history. Keeps Superhuman data separate from core Contact fields. The most common choice among 1mind customers. | Requires HubSpot Enterprise and additional setup, including making records easy for sellers to find from the Contact. |

Many teams use both: a few key fields on the Contact for quick reference, and full detail on the custom object. 1mind Services can review your HubSpot account with you to determine the best fit.

#### Recommended fields

All fields are optional, but **we strongly recommend creating dedicated fields to capture conversation data**. Without them, conversation data has nowhere to land in HubSpot, and sellers would need to read full transcripts to understand each conversation.

The most valuable fields are the conversation summary, key topics discussed, and pain points. 1mind can also derive other information that matters to your team, such as competitors mentioned, timeline, budget, or suggested follow-up questions for the seller. Create a property for it in HubSpot, and 1mind Services will include it in the integration.

Answers the visitor gives in conversation, such as the questions your demo-request form would normally ask, are written to the Contact fields you choose.

The tables below are a suggested starting point. Your final field list is confirmed in the field mapping worksheet.

**Contact properties**

Create these in a `1mind` property group on the Contact. Each property holds data from the most recent conversation.

| Property | Type | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `onemind_visitor_id` | Single-line text | 1mind visitor ID |
| `onemind_last_conversation_id` | Single-line text | ID of the most recent conversation |
| `onemind_last_conversation_at` | Date and time | When the most recent conversation ended |
| `onemind_qualification_status` | Dropdown | Conversation Signal, such as Qualified, Nurture, Disqualified, Support, or No Outcome |
| `onemind_meeting_booked` | Checkbox | True only when the scheduler confirms a booking |
| `onemind_last_transcript` | Multi-line text | Full transcript of the most recent conversation |
| `onemind_last_summary` | Multi-line text | Short summary for sellers |
| `onemind_last_pain_points` | Multi-line text | Pain points the visitor described |
| Custom fields | Any | Any additional information you want derived from the conversation |

The qualification status reflects the conversation's **Conversation Signal**. For example, **Qualified** means the visitor met your qualification criteria during the conversation.

**1mind Conversation custom object (optional, requires HubSpot Enterprise)**

One record per conversation, associated to the Contact.

| Property | Type | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `onemind_conversation_id` | Single-line text, unique | Conversation ID |
| `onemind_visitor_id` | Single-line text | 1mind visitor ID |
| `started_at` / `ended_at` | Date and time | Conversation start and end |
| `qualification_status` | Dropdown | Same values as the Contact property |
| `transcript` | Multi-line text | Full conversation transcript |
| `summary` | Multi-line text | Short summary for sellers |
| `product_interest`, `pain_points`, `objections`, `next_action` | Multi-line text | Captured only from what the visitor said |
| `meeting_booked` / `meeting_id` | Checkbox / text | Populated only when the scheduler confirms a booking |
| `source_url`, `utm_campaign` | Single-line text | Page and campaign where the conversation started |

<Info>
  You'll need to share the custom object Id with 1mind Services 
</Info>

#### Creating and updating Contacts

You define how 1mind handles existing Contacts, **field by field**:

- **Fill empty fields only:** Common for standard fields such as first name, last name, and email.
- **Overwrite with what the visitor said:** Useful for fields that go out of date, such as job title.
- **Conditional rules:** Rules can reference other HubSpot fields. For example, if `customer_type` is Partner, leave the Contact unchanged and log the conversation only on the 1mind Conversation record.

Visitors who share an email address can be created as new Contacts if you allow it.

If you use HubSpot alongside Salesforce or another CRM, decide which system creates new people first. This prevents duplicate records and keeps your routing intact.

| If | Then 1mind |
| --- | --- |
| HubSpot owns lead creation and assignment | Writes the Contact (and the conversation record, if used) directly to HubSpot |
| Salesforce or another CRM owns lead assignment | Writes to that CRM first. Your existing sync brings the data into HubSpot, and 1mind does not also create the person in HubSpot. |

1mind does not change owner, lifecycle stage, lead status, lead score, or deal stage unless you request it. Your HubSpot workflows continue to handle downstream routing, alerts, nurture, etc. 

### Using 1mind data in HubSpot

Because conversation data is stored in standard HubSpot properties and records, you can use it anywhere HubSpot supports filtering:

- **Workflows:** For example, when `onemind_qualification_status` is Qualified, notify the owner and create a follow-up task. When it's Nurture and the Contact has marketing consent, enroll them in a nurture sequence. When it's Support, route to your support team and skip sales alerts.
- **Lists and segments:** Build lists of Contacts who spoke with the Superhuman, booked a meeting, or asked about a specific product.
- **Reports:** Report on conversations, qualification outcomes, and meetings by campaign or source page.

<Tip>
  If a workflow depends on the 1mind Conversation record, trigger it from that record rather than from the Contact. The Contact can update slightly before the conversation record is written.
</Tip>

### Disconnecting HubSpot

To stop syncing, go to **Integrations → CRM → HubSpot** in the 1mind Console and select **Disconnect**.

To remove the 1mind app from HubSpot entirely, uninstall it from **Connected Apps** in HubSpot. See [HubSpot's guide to uninstalling apps](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketplace/install-apps-in-the-hubspot-marketplace#uninstall-an-app).

After you disconnect, new conversations no longer sync to HubSpot. Existing Contact properties and 1mind Conversation records remain in HubSpot.

### FAQs and troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will my sellers need to read the full transcript?">
    No. The recommended fields, such as the summary, key topics, and pain points, give sellers the highlights at a glance. The full transcript is available when they need more detail. If access to the 1mind console is granted to your seller, they can view the transcript in the 1mind Console. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will the Superhuman greet visitors by name?">
    Only if you allow it. You choose field by field which HubSpot values the Superhuman can reference. All other values are used only in the background and written back to HubSpot.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the conversation replace a short form?">
    Yes. If your form asks only a few questions, the Superhuman can collect the answers in conversation and write them to HubSpot afterward, just like a form submission.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How quickly can routing changes be made?">
    If you use HubSpot routing, changes you make in HubSpot take effect immediately. If 1mind routes to your HubSpot Meetings links, send the change to 1mind Services. Changes are typically completed within three to five business days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why wasn't a Contact created or updated after a conversation?">
    Confirm that the visitor provided a trusted identifier. Anonymous visitors who never share an email address don't create or update a Contact. Contact 1mind Services to initiate an investigation into the session. Provide the 1mind Session Id or timeframe. 
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why am I seeing duplicate Contacts?">
    This usually happens when both 1mind and a CRM sync create the same person. Review which system creates people first under [Creating and updating Contacts](#creating-and-updating-contacts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why didn't my workflow run?">
    Compare the workflow's enrollment criteria to the exact values 1mind writes, including capitalization. If the workflow is triggered by the Contact but depends on data from the conversation record, trigger it from the 1mind Conversation record instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why aren't 1mind Conversation records appearing?">
    This applies only if you use the optional custom object. Confirm that your account is on HubSpot Enterprise, the custom object exists with the expected property internal names, and the Conversation-to-Contact association is enabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why wasn't a HubSpot form submitted?">
    Form submission is optional. Confirm that it's enabled for your Superhuman and that the connection was approved with the `forms` scope.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The integration was working and has stopped.">
    The app may have been uninstalled in HubSpot, or the user who connected it may have lost access. Contact 1mind Services to reconnect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For help, contact 1mind Services with the conversation ID, and the approximate time of the conversation.

## Ride-Along

The 1mind HubSpot integration connects your Ride-Along Superhuman to HubSpot before and after every call. Ride-Along joins a scheduled or live call alongside your rep, solutions engineer, CSM, or support specialist, and a single call can include several members of your team and several customer attendees.

The integration runs two workflows:

- **Pre-call workflow:** Retrieves CRM context from HubSpot to prepare a meeting briefing for your team.
- **Post-call workflow:** Writes selected conversation outcomes back to HubSpot.

<Info>
  1mind Services builds each integration with your team. The objects, fields, matching rules, associations, and permitted updates are defined by your configured workflows.
</Info>

### Ride-Along decisions

Your answers to these questions shape how your Ride-Along integration is built.

| Decision | Where it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Which Ride-Along profile you use: Sales Engineer, CS Onboarding, or Tech Support | [How the Ride-Along integration works](#how-the-ride-along-integration-works) |
| Whether your rep selects HubSpot records before each call, or 1mind matches attendees automatically | [Before the call](#before-the-call) |
| Which speaking mode Ride-Along uses: Active, Passive, or Silent | [During the call](#during-the-call) |
| Whether outcomes are written to standard HubSpot records, custom objects, or both | [Where Ride-Along data lives](#where-ride-along-data-lives) |
| Which outcomes to capture, and your launch tier: MVP, Advanced, or Comprehensive | [Recommended Ride-Along fields](#recommended-ride-along-fields) |
| Which teams can see sensitive fields | [Record access](#record-access) |

### Ride-Along requirements

- A HubSpot **Super Admin**, or a user who can install apps, create properties, and (if used) create custom objects and associations.
- Admin access to your 1mind workspace.
- A HubSpot **sandbox** account (strongly recommended). 1mind connects to your sandbox first, then to production once testing is complete.

<Note>
  The optional RIDEALONG and RIDEALONG ATTENDEE custom objects require a **HubSpot Enterprise** subscription with available custom-object capacity.
</Note>

#### Ride-Along permissions

The connection requests the following HubSpot scopes:

| Scope | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oauth` | Yes | Standard HubSpot app connection |
| `crm.objects.contacts.read` | Yes | Read attendee Contacts for the pre-call briefing |
| `crm.objects.companies.read` | Yes | Read the account for the pre-call briefing |
| `crm.objects.deals.read` | Optional | Required only if the briefing includes Deal context, such as with the Sales Engineer profile |
| `crm.objects.custom.read` | Optional | Required only for the custom objects. Finds existing Ride-Along records to prevent duplicates. |
| `crm.objects.custom.write` | Optional | Required only for the custom objects. Creates Ride-Along records and their associations. |
| `crm.schemas.custom.read` | Optional | Required only for the custom objects. Reads the two Ride-Along object schemas. |

If your post-call workflow updates standard records such as Contacts, Companies, or Deals, the matching write scopes are also required.

### How the Ride-Along integration works

| Phase | When | What 1mind does with HubSpot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Before the call** | The meeting is scheduled or about to start | Reads the relevant Contacts, Company, Deal, and owner to prepare a briefing for your team |
| **During the call** | Your team and the customer are meeting | Uses the briefing to assist your team. Seller-only CRM context is never shown to prospects. |
| **After the call** | The meeting has ended | Writes the outcomes you've selected back to HubSpot: standard records, the optional custom objects, or both |

Your Ride-Along profile (Sales Engineer, CS Onboarding, or Tech Support) determines what's included in the briefing and which outcomes are captured. For example, the Sales Engineer profile includes Deal context and deal progression recommendations.

### Before the call

The pre-call workflow identifies the HubSpot records related to the meeting and reads the fields you approve. There are two ways to identify them:

- **Manual launch:** Your rep selects the Contact, Company, and Deal before the call.
- **Automatic matching:** 1mind matches attendees to HubSpot Contacts by exact email address. Ambiguous matches are left unmatched rather than guessed.

#### What 1mind reads

| Object | Example fields |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | `hs_object_id`, `email`, `firstname`, `lastname`, `jobtitle` |
| Company | `hs_object_id`, `name` |
| Deal | `hs_object_id`, `dealname`, `pipeline`, `dealstage`, `amount`, `closedate`, `hs_next_step`, `description` |

Your final list of fields is confirmed with 1mind Services.

### During the call

Your rep leads the meeting. Ride-Along assists using the context gathered before the call, in the speaking mode you choose: Active, Passive, or Silent.

Seller-only CRM context from the briefing is never shared with prospects.

### After the call

When the call ends, the post-call workflow writes the outcomes you've selected back to HubSpot.

#### Where Ride-Along data lives

| Option | Benefits | Considerations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard HubSpot records** | Uses the Contacts, Companies, and Deals your team already works in. Available on any HubSpot plan. | Holds only the most recent call's values in each field, and mixes Ride-Along data with your core fields. |
| **RIDEALONG and RIDEALONG ATTENDEE custom objects** | One record per meeting and one per participant, preserving full history. Captures what each attendee cared about. Keeps Ride-Along data separate from core fields. | Requires HubSpot Enterprise and additional setup, including associations to Contacts and Companies. |

With the custom objects, every participant receives an attendee record, even without a matching Contact. Unmatched participants are never created as new Contacts. You can optionally associate meetings to the Deal so they appear on the Deal record.

#### Recommended Ride-Along fields

The tables below are the MVP starting point. Advanced and Comprehensive tiers add further fields, and your final field list is confirmed with 1mind Services.

**RIDEALONG (one per meeting)**

| Property | Type | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ridealong_key` | Single-line text, unique | Provider and meeting ID, for example `zoom:83920123456` |
| `meeting_provider`, `provider_meeting_id`, `meeting_title` | Single-line text | Meeting details |
| `meeting_start_time`, `meeting_end_time` | Date and time | When the call took place |
| `summary`, `decisions`, `next_steps`, `action_items` | Multi-line text | What happened and who owns each next step |
| `deal_progression_plan`, `critical_unknowns_validation_plan` | Multi-line text | Recommendations for the rep to review |
| `customer_follow_up_email_draft`, `next_meeting_strategy` | Multi-line text | Drafts only. Never sent automatically. |

**RIDEALONG ATTENDEE (one per participant)**

| Property | Type | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `attendance_key` | Single-line text, unique | Meeting key plus participant ID |
| `attendee_email`, `attendee_full_name` | Single-line text | Who attended |
| `person_type`, `meeting_role` | Single-line text | For example, Prospect or Internal; Host or Evaluator |
| `attended` | Checkbox | Whether the participant joined |
| `stakeholder_perspective`, `recommended_rep_approach` | Multi-line text | What this person cared about in the meeting, and how to follow up |

#### Updating standard records

If your post-call workflow writes to Contacts, Companies, or Deals, you define the rules field by field, such as filling empty fields only or overwriting with the latest call's outcome.

<Note>
  Ride-Along only writes the updates your workflows are configured to make. It never sends email: follow-up email drafts remain drafts for your rep to edit and send.
</Note>

#### Record access

Recording URLs, transcript URLs, and attendee profile fields (such as sentiment, buying role, and champion likelihood) can be sensitive. Restrict them in HubSpot to the teams that should see them.

### Using Ride-Along data in HubSpot

- **Deal and account reviews:** See every meeting, its decisions, and next steps from the Contact, Company, or Deal record.
- **Workflows:** For example, create tasks from action items, or alert the owner when a meeting surfaces a new stakeholder.
- **Reports:** Report on meetings by account, Deal, or meeting type, and track who attended.
