This guide covers how an admin sets up calendar routing end to end, so meetings booked through your Superhuman reach the right rep. It is the setup companion to the [Calendars integration](/configuring/integrations/calendars) and the [Calendar routing](/using/sub-agents/calendar-routing) reference.

## Who does what

- **Reps (Member role)** connect their own calendar and meeting provider, set their availability, and opt in to auto-join where the workspace allows it. Every role manages its own scheduling profile.
- **Admins (Workspace Admin or Super Admin)** invite reps, create meeting types and teams, manage round-robin, and configure auto-join for a workspace. These two roles are the only ones with the Scheduling and Meetings permission.
- **Org admins (Super Admin)** turn auto-join on for the organization, which has to happen before any workspace can use it.
- **1mind Services** builds and validates the routing workflow with you.

For the full workspace roles table, see the roles reference.

## The setup sequence

1. **The admin invites reps** into the workspace.
2. **Each rep connects** their own calendar and meeting provider, and sets availability. A rep who has not connected both a calendar and a meeting provider is skipped by routing, with no warning, so confirm connections before go-live.
3. **The admin creates meeting types and teams**, including any round-robin teams.
4. **The customer writes down its routing rules** and gives them to Services (see below).
5. **Services builds the routing and validates it** with you before launch.

## What to give Services in writing

Before routing can be built, give Services your routing rules in writing: which meeting types exist, which teams or reps each type should route to, the round-robin or priority order within a team, and any fallback when no one matches.

## Check which reps have connected

Routing skips a rep who has not connected, and it does not warn you, so check connections before go-live and again afterward. Each team's member list shows who has connected a calendar, and so does the **Add Teammate** picker when you build a team.

Admins cannot connect a rep's calendar for them. Each rep connects their own.

## Practices Product recommends

- Keep a **backstop team** so a meeting still routes when no primary match is available.
- Use **one team per routing branch**, so each decision in your rules maps to a single team.
- **Check connections before launch**, and after any change to the team.
