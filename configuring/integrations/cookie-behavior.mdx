<Warning>
  **Cookies are not required.** The 1mind script loads and the Superhuman runs fully without dropping any cookies. You do not need to allow cookie-dropping for 1mind to work — there is no dependency.
</Warning>

## First-party, not third-party

1mind's launcher runs in your page's context, so any cookies it sets are **first-party on your domain** — not third-party trackers, and never synced to ad networks.

## The cookies that may be set (both optional)

| Cookie | Set when | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `om_user_token` | Only when the **remember-user** feature is enabled | Recognize a returning visitor so the conversation can resume context across sessions |
| `_zitok` | Only when **ZoomInfo enrichment** is enabled | Support IP/firmographic enrichment |

Both are entirely opt-in and customer-controlled. With neither enabled, the Superhuman still functions fully.

## Consent model

The launcher loads behind your existing **CMP / tag-manager consent gate** (for example OneTrust or Cookiebot). Your data-privacy team controls how 1mind's cookies are classified (Functional/Required vs. Non-essential) within your CMP.

If a visitor declines cookies, there are two graceful paths:

- The Superhuman runs in **limited-memory mode** (no cross-session persistence), or
- The widget **waits for the CMP consent signal** before initializing.

## What 1mind does not do

- No third-party tracking cookies.
- No identity sync to advertising networks.
- No dependency on cookie-dropping for the script to load or function.
