A **team** in 1mind is a group of reps who share incoming meetings through round-robin. Only **Workspace Admins** and **Super Admins** can manage teams. This page explains how the queue picks the next rep and how to keep the split fair.

## How the queue picks the next rep

Every rep on a team has a **Level**, and the rep with the lowest Level is up next. Level is driven by two things:

- **Events**: how many meetings they have already received this cycle.
- **Weight**: how big a share they are supposed to get.

Level is Events divided by Weight (plus any Credit or Skip you have applied), so a rep on 50% Weight climbs twice as fast per meeting as a rep on 100%. When two reps show the same Level, the one who has gone longer since their last meeting wins.

## Weight

Weight is a percentage, and **100% is the standard share**. Set a rep above 100% to give them more meetings than a typical rep; set them below 100% to give them fewer. For example, 200% roughly doubles a rep's volume over time and 50% roughly halves it.

A Weight change takes effect **immediately** and recalculates the rep's place in line using their existing history. A rep can move from last to first the moment you save a new Weight. That is expected, not a bug.

## Credit and Skip

When a rep's Level does not reflect reality, **Credit** and **Skip** adjust one rep by hand without touching anyone else:

- **Credit** moves a rep **up** (sooner). Use it to undo meetings that should not have counted, such as spam leads.
- **Skip** moves a rep **down** (later). Use it for extra meetings they should not have received, or to delay their next one.

Enter an exact number and apply it in one step; you do not click one at a time. For example, a row showing 3 spam bookings takes `Credit: 3` to move back to where it would have been.

How far a Credit or Skip moves a rep depends on their Weight: the same number shifts a lower-Weight rep further than a higher-Weight rep. To land on an exact spot, click into the **Level** value and type the target number directly.

## Add a rep to a team

A new rep starts near the team's **current average Level**, so they neither jump the line nor wait forever for a first meeting. For example, with the team at Levels 4.0, 4.0, 5.0, 5.3, and 6.0, a new rep starts at about 4.9.

A new rep receives no meetings until they have connected **both** a calendar and a meeting provider, even when it is their turn.

## Preview a change with Simulate

**Simulate** on the team page projects a number of future meetings forward using the current Levels and Weights, and shows the resulting split before you commit. Use it to sanity-check a Weight change or to see when a rep who is behind will start getting meetings again.

## Shared queue

Team scheduling-link bookings and Superhuman bookings share one round-robin queue per team, so a booking from either source affects the order for both.

## Quick reference

| Action | What it does | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a team member | Starts them near the team's average Level; no meetings until calendar and meeting provider are connected | Onboarding a rep to a team |
| Adjust Weight | Changes their ongoing share vs. the 100% baseline; takes effect immediately | Giving a top performer more volume, or easing in a new hire |
| Credit | Moves a rep sooner | Spam leads or other one-off corrections in the rep's favor |
| Skip | Moves a rep later | An extra meeting they should not have received, or delaying their next |
| Simulate | Projects future meetings forward and shows the split | Sanity-checking a Weight change |
