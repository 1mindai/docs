## What "behind the form" means

For website use cases (inbound lead capture, demand gen, BDR), one of the
highest-performing placements is to show a Superhuman *after* a form is submitted.
Instead of a static "Thank You" page, the Superhuman picks up the conversation
immediately, using the data the prospect just submitted to personalize the
experience.

Setting this up comes down to four decisions.

## 1. Where the Superhuman appears after submission

Two standard options:

- **Replaces the form in place** — after submission, the Superhuman appears where the form was; the fields disappear and the Superhuman takes over.
- **Redirects to a "Thank You" page** — the user is sent to a confirmation page where the Superhuman is embedded and ready to engage.

Your web or marketing-ops team usually knows which fits your flow. Confirm early —
it affects how the launcher script is configured.

## 2. How form inputs are passed to the Superhuman

For the Superhuman to reference what the prospect submitted (name, company, use
case), the data must be passed from the form into the Superhuman. Two supported
methods:

**Option A — Custom data attributes via the launcher script (preferred).** When
the 1mind script initializes on the page, custom data attributes are populated with
the values the user entered. Your dev/marketing-ops team sets it up so the launcher
is (re)initialized with those values injected on the post-submit state. Best for
in-place replacements or when you control script initialization. See
[Customizing with attributes](/configuring/integrations/website-embed) for the
implementation pattern.

**Option B — URL parameters after submission.** After submission, your site
appends URL parameters (populated with the form values) to the destination URL
where the Superhuman is embedded; 1mind reads them when the conversation starts.
Best for redirect-to-thank-you setups. Your team must append the parameters
reliably and agree field names with 1mind in advance.

<Note>
  If both are possible, Option A (data attributes) is generally preferred for
  reliability and cleanliness.
</Note>

## 3. Attribution identifier

Confirm whether there's a **form submission ID, UTM parameter, or other unique
identifier** 1mind should capture alongside the conversation. This matters for:

- Tying conversation data back to the original form submission in your CRM/MAP
- Attribution reporting (connecting a Superhuman-influenced conversation to a lead or campaign)
- Auditing/troubleshooting a specific submission

If yes, share the parameter name(s) and how they're generated (e.g., Marketo
Munchkin ID, Salesforce campaign ID, UTM source/medium).

## 4. Conversation values vs. metadata

Not every field needs to appear in the live conversation. Confirm which fields are:

- **Conversation values** — actively referenced/personalized during the conversation (e.g., first name, company, use-case interest).
- **Metadata only** — captured and stored but not spoken or referenced (e.g., hidden fields, campaign IDs, lead-scoring values).

This helps configure the conversation logic correctly and avoids surfacing
irrelevant or sensitive data.
