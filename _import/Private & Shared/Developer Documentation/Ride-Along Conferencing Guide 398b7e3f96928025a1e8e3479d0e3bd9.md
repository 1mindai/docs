# Ride-Along Conferencing Guide

# **Your Ride-Along Superhuman on Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams**

Updated July 8, 2026

The 1mind Ride-Along Superhuman is an AI sales engineer that joins your live calls as a full participant: on camera, with a voice, and with deep knowledge of your product. It listens to the whole room and speaks when your team calls on it to handle technical questions, security objections, and product deep dives. And it now works everywhere your customers meet.

---

## What’s new

This page replaces the earlier Zoom App documentation. Since then, the Ride-Along platform has been rebuilt and expanded in nearly every direction:

- **Three platforms** - Google Meet and Microsoft Teams join Zoom as fully supported platforms. One Superhuman, every meeting link.
- **Calendar scheduling** - Your Superhuman has its own email address. Add it to a calendar invite and it RSVPs, reminds you, and joins on time.
- **Screen understanding (coming soon)** - When anyone shares a screen, the Superhuman will be able to see it and speak to exactly what is being shown. This capability is in internal testing today and is not yet enabled on customer accounts. Your 1mind team will confirm when it is available for your workspace.
- **Multi-speaker hearing** - Every participant is heard as a distinct voice, so it knows who said what in a crowded room.
- **Meeting chat Q&A** - Attendees can ask questions in the meeting chat and get expert answers without interrupting the conversation.
- **Multilingual calls (Beta)** - Pick the language for each meeting. Disclosures and chat replies are translated automatically.
- **CRM briefings** - Attach the Salesforce or HubSpot account, opportunity, and contacts so it walks in knowing the deal.
- **A richer console** - Upcoming, Live, and History tabs with live statuses, co-hosts, invitees, briefing sheets, and summaries.

> **No app install required.** Earlier versions relied on a Zoom Marketplace app. The Superhuman now joins as a standard meeting participant on all three platforms. There is nothing for you, your team, or your customers to download, install, or authorize in the meeting itself.

## What the Ride-Along Superhuman does

- **Expert backup on every call** - Precise answers on security, architecture, integrations, and product capabilities, delivered in the moment the question is asked. Coverage across every motion, from BDR-led calls to late-stage technical evaluations.
- **A polite participant, not a wildcard** - By default it joins silently and speaks only when invited. Your team decides, per meeting and even mid-meeting, how forward it should be.
- **Aware of the whole meeting** - It hears each speaker as a distinct voice, reads the meeting chat, and carries the briefing you gave it: context notes, files, and CRM data.
- **An active presenter when you want one** - It can share slides, play product videos, and walk through live web content directly in the meeting, while you keep hold-and-stop controls in the console.

## Platforms: Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams

The Superhuman joins meetings on all three platforms with the same core experience: it appears as a named, on-camera participant, posts its AI disclosure in the chat as it arrives, and behaves according to the engagement mode you chose. Capabilities are consistent across platforms, with one exception: private (direct) chat messages are a Zoom-only feature, so host welcome messages and private replies fall back to the public meeting chat elsewhere.

| Capability | Zoom | Google Meet | Microsoft Teams |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Join instantly by meeting link | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Join from a calendar invite | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| On-camera presence with live avatar | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Hears each speaker separately | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Sees shared screens (coming soon) | Coming soon | Coming soon | Coming soon |
| Presents slides, video, and live web content | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| AI disclosure and replies in meeting chat | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Answers attendee questions in chat (Chat Q&A) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Private messages to hosts (welcome notes, direct replies) | Zoom only | Public chat | Public chat |
| Waits politely in waiting rooms and lobbies | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Meeting links are recognized automatically, including links embedded in calendar invites (zoom.us, meet.google.com, teams.microsoft.com, and teams.live.com).

### Admission works like any external guest

The Superhuman enters through the front door. Zoom waiting rooms, Microsoft Teams lobbies, and Google Meet’s “ask to join” flow all behave normally: the host admits it once and it joins. If your organization restricts guest or external participants, those platform policies apply to the Superhuman too, so it is worth confirming them with your meeting-platform admin before an important call.

## Two ways to bring your Superhuman to a meeting

### Instant: paste a meeting link

From the Conferencing page in your 1mind console, choose **Add to meeting**, paste any Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams link, and it joins in seconds. Before it joins you can:

- Name the meeting and set the engagement mode
- Add context notes and attach files
- Pick the meeting language (when multilingual is enabled)
- Add co-hosts and toggle chat Q&A

### Scheduled: invite it like a teammate

Every Ride-Along Superhuman has its own email address, shown at the top of your Conferencing page (for example `superhuman-a1b2c3@1mind.app`, or a friendly alias on your own domain). Add that address to a calendar event, or forward the invite to it, and:

- It replies to the invite, so Accepted or Declined shows right in your calendar
- The meeting appears in your **Upcoming** tab, ready to be briefed
- The organizer gets a reminder email shortly before start
- It joins on time, in the mode you set

### Scheduling details worth knowing

- **Whoever forwards the invite owns the meeting** in the console and is automatically added as a host.
- **Recurring meetings are supported**: daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly series, including edited or cancelled single occurrences. You can also forward just one occurrence of a series.
- **Reschedules and cancellations sync when the Superhuman receives the calendar update.** Add it to the actual event as an attendee. If you only forwarded the original invitation, forward subsequent changes or cancellations too.
- **All-day events are not supported**, and if an invite cannot be scheduled (no meeting link, an unsupported link, a past event), you get an email explaining exactly why.

## Engagement modes: you decide how forward it is

Each meeting has an engagement mode, set before the call and changeable at any moment during it. Mode changes apply instantly and are attributed in the meeting record, so there is never a question of who changed what.

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Silent** (default) | Will not speak at all. It still listens, watches shared screens, and can answer questions in the meeting chat if chat Q&A is on. The safest setting for calls where the AI should observe and support quietly. |
| **Standby** | Listens until activated by a wake word. Say its name when you want it to weigh in, and it returns to listening when the moment passes. Available on request for your workspace. |
| **Active** | Actively participates, no wake words required. It will jump in on its own when it can add value. In Active mode you can also give it an opening introduction to deliver when it joins. |

### Four ways to switch modes mid-call

- **Console:** one click on the live meeting screen.
- **Chat commands:** hosts can type `/active`, `/standby`, or `/silent` in the meeting chat.
- **Natural language:** hosts can simply write “you can jump in now” or “go quiet please” in chat.
- **Wake words:** in Standby, saying its name invites it to speak.

## Brief it like you would a colleague

Open any upcoming meeting to see its briefing sheet. Everything below can be set when you create an instant meeting too, and most of it can still be adjusted while the call is live.

- **Context notes** - Free-form guidance for this specific session: goals, product focus, competitive landscape, landmines to avoid, or anything the Superhuman should prioritize.
- **Files and documents** - Attach decks, one-pagers, pricing sheets, and docs (PDF, PowerPoint, Word, text, images, video; up to 25 MB per file, 100 files per meeting). A readiness indicator confirms when every file has been processed and is available to the AI. Files can be added or removed mid-meeting.
- **CRM context: Salesforce and HubSpot** - Associate the account, opportunity, and contacts for the deal. For scheduled meetings, a research pass runs automatically about 30 minutes before start and prepares deal-aware context, with your operator in review of what the AI receives.
- **Meeting language (Beta)** - Choose from the languages your Superhuman persona speaks and understands. The AI disclosure, chat replies, and its voice follow the selected language.
- **Co-hosts** - Add teammates as hosts so they can manage the meeting too: switch modes, send instructions, upload documents, and message it privately. The organizer is always a host; invitees can be promoted in one click.
- **Who’s invited** - For calendar-scheduled meetings, the attendee list is read from the invite, with roles (organizer, required, optional) shown in the console so you know exactly who the Superhuman expects in the room.

## What it can do in the meeting

### It hears the room, not a recording of the room

Audio is processed per participant in real time, so the Superhuman follows multi-person conversations naturally: it knows the difference between your AE thinking out loud and the customer’s security lead asking a pointed question.

### It will see what is shared (coming soon)

Screen understanding is in internal testing and is not yet enabled on customer accounts. Once available, the Superhuman will watch the active presenter's screen live: bring up an architecture diagram and ask it to explain the data flow, or show a pricing page and let it handle the packaging question. Screen understanding will be continuous, so it reacts to what is on screen at the time. Until then, the Superhuman participates on audio, meeting chat, and the briefing material you give it. Talk to your 1mind team if you would like to be considered for early access.

### It can present, too

The Superhuman can share slide decks, play product videos, and walk through live web content directly in the meeting. From the console, the organizer keeps direct control: **Hold content** pins the current slide, and **Stop sharing** ends the share immediately, no AI interpretation required.

### The meeting chat is a second channel

With chat Q&A enabled, attendees can ask questions in the meeting chat and get expert answers there, without ever interrupting the speaker. The chat brain shares full context with the voice brain, so the two surfaces answer as one entity. Hosts also get commands:

| In the chat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/active` `/standby` `/silent` | Switches the engagement mode (hosts only, with a confirmation reply). |
| `/message <note>` | Sends the Superhuman a private note it works into its next turn. |
| `/urgent <note>` | Interrupts it immediately with your instruction. |
| Plain language | Hosts can steer it conversationally: “wrap up this topic”, “you can jump in now”. |
| Questions from attendees | Answered in chat when chat Q&A is on. On Zoom, hosts can also DM it privately. |

### Real-time instructions from the console

The live meeting screen has an instruction box with two send options: **Act now** interrupts the Superhuman with your guidance immediately, and **Next turn** applies it the next time it speaks. Use it to redirect the conversation, feed it an answer, or tell it to stand down, all invisible to the other side.

### Live status, always visible

The console shows exactly where the Superhuman is in its lifecycle: Joining, In waiting room, Live, Rejoining, Host ended, Removed, Invalid link. If it cannot get in, the reason is specific: meeting locked, wrong password, sign-in required, registration required, or an invalid or expired link.

### Leave, rejoin, remove

- **Rejoin** brings it back into a live meeting with full memory of the conversation so far, and you choose the mode (and whether it greets) on the way back in.
- **Remove from meeting** takes it off the call from the console; meeting hosts can also mute or remove it with the platform’s native controls, like any participant.
- Rejoining keeps everything under a single meeting record, so history stays clean.

## The console: Upcoming, Live, and Meeting history

The Conferencing page in your 1mind console is mission control, with your Superhuman’s calendar email up top and three tabs below:

- **Upcoming** - Every scheduled meeting, who scheduled it, whether it has been briefed, and a pulsing “Starting soon” badge inside two minutes of start. Open any row to brief or cancel.
- **Live** - Meetings in flight, with live status, mode controls, real-time instructions, screen share controls, documents, hosts, and the chat Q&A switch.
- **Meeting history** - Past meetings with duration, outcome, and an automatic meeting summary. Jump into Engagement Explorer for the full conversation review.

Plus:

- **Role-aware visibility:** members see their own meetings; admins see the workspace’s; hosts see meetings they co-host.
- **Filters that keep up:** filter any tab by host, and history by date, with pagination for busy teams.
- **Meeting outcomes are honest:** history distinguishes a meeting the host ended from one where the Superhuman was removed or the meeting was cancelled, and failed joins say why.

## Everyone knows it’s an AI, and you decide what it does

- **It announces itself.** The moment it joins, a disclosure message posts to the meeting chat. The default is “Superhuman AI has joined the meeting”; you can customize it per deployment, and it is automatically translated into the languages your persona speaks.
- **It is visibly present.** It joins under a clear name you configure (for example “Superhuman Alex AI”), with its camera on. There is no hidden listener mode.
- **It respects the room.** It waits in waiting rooms and lobbies until admitted, does not greet unprompted, and in its default mode does not speak at all.
- **Hosts stay in charge.** Meeting hosts can mute it or remove it using their platform’s normal controls, and your team can pull it out from the console at any time.
- **It only says and shows what you’ve approved.** The Superhuman draws on the knowledge base your team configures for it, presents only materials it has been given, and follows guardrails for sensitive topics such as pricing and competitive questions.
- **Only your team can send it in.** Inviting and controlling a Superhuman requires an authorized user in your 1mind console. No one outside your workspace can add it to a meeting.

## Getting started: from license to live call

1. **Activate your Ride-Along deployment.** An active 1mind license is required. Your 1mind team configures the deployment with you: the Superhuman’s display name, its AI disclosure message, its calendar email (for a custom-domain alias, your email/domain administrator sets up forwarding and the same alias must also be entered in the Console), and workspace options such as document uploads, chat Q&A, and multilingual support. See the email-address FAQ below for the current Production alias-input regression.
2. **Open Conferencing in your 1mind console.** You will find your Superhuman’s email address at the top with a one-click copy button, plus the Upcoming, Live, and Meeting history tabs.
3. **Put it on a call.** Paste a meeting link for an instant join, or add its email address to any calendar invite. It RSVPs and appears in Upcoming.
4. **Brief it.** Set the engagement mode, add context, attach files, link the CRM opportunity, pick the language, and add co-hosts.
5. **Run the call your way.** Let it observe in Silent, wake it by name in Standby, or let it participate in Active. Steer it live through the console or the meeting chat.
6. **Review afterwards.** Read the summary in Meeting history and dig into the full conversation in Engagement Explorer.

## Troubleshooting: when something looks off

| What you see | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| In waiting room | The Superhuman is waiting to be let in. Ask the meeting host to admit it; it will wait patiently and join the moment it is admitted. |
| Invalid link | The meeting link is malformed, expired, or points to a meeting that does not exist. Double-check the link and try again; scheduling rejections also arrive by email with the specific reason. |
| Meeting locked | The host has locked the meeting. Ask them to unlock it, then use Rejoin. |
| Sign-in or registration required | The meeting requires authenticated or registered participants. Loosen that requirement for this meeting, or use a link that allows guests. |
| Wrong password | The link’s embedded passcode is missing or incorrect. Re-copy the full invite link, including the passcode. |
| Not speaking when expected | Check the engagement mode: Silent never speaks, and Standby waits for its wake word. Switch to Active in one click, or type `/active` in the meeting chat. |
| No answers in the meeting chat | Chat Q&A may be off for this meeting or for the deployment. Flip the switch on the live meeting screen, or enable it when briefing. |
| Blurry camera in Microsoft Teams | Teams controls the camera resolution requested from the Superhuman. Speaking or pinning does not guarantee higher resolution, and no override is currently available. See the [**Joining & In-Call Control**](https://app.notion.com/p/Joining-In-Call-Control-2e5b7e3f969280beb157c1fde213b628?pvs=21) for confirmed limitations and support guidance. |

## Limits and fine print

- Session documents: up to 25 MB per file and 100 files per meeting.
- Meeting chat replies respect platform message limits; longer answers arrive as a short series of messages.
- Recurring series expand on daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly rules; sub-hourly recurrence and all-day events are not supported.
- Up to 50 overlapping scheduled meetings per workspace at any one time.
- Private chat (DMs) is available on Zoom; on Google Meet and Microsoft Teams the Superhuman uses the public meeting chat.
- Screen understanding (coming soon) will follow one active presenter at a time.
- Multilingual calls are in beta and limited to the languages your persona is configured to speak and understand.

## Built for security review

- **Compliance posture** - 1mind maintains SOC 2 compliance, aligns with GDPR and CCPA, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and undergoes regular penetration testing. Details and reports are available through our [Trust Center](https://trust.1mind.com/).
- **Nothing to install** - The Superhuman joins as a standard meeting participant. No plugins, browser extensions, marketplace apps, or OAuth grants are required for anyone in the meeting.
- **Authenticated media channels** - Real-time audio and screen streams flow over channels authenticated with per-session, expiring signed tokens. Event callbacks from our conferencing infrastructure are signature-verified before they are trusted.
- **Data minimization** - Meeting audio and video are processed in real time so the Superhuman can participate, not to build an archive. Individual participant video is never stored, post-call data handling is governed by your data agreement with 1mind, and content the Superhuman presents is validated before it is ever shared to the room.

## FAQ

**Do meeting participants need to install anything?**
No. The Superhuman joins like any other participant. Your customers just see a named, on-camera attendee with an AI disclosure in the chat.

**What happens with waiting rooms and lobbies?**
It waits. The console shows “In waiting room” with a prompt to ask the host to admit it, and it joins the moment it is let in. It is never cancelled just for waiting.

**Can more than one person manage it during a call?**
Yes. Add teammates as co-hosts and they get the full live console: modes, instructions, documents, and chat controls. Hosts can also steer it from the meeting chat with commands or plain language.

**Can we control what it says and shows?**
Yes. It answers from the knowledge and content your team has approved, guardrails cover sensitive topics, and hosts can change its mode or remove it at any moment, from the console or right in the meeting chat.

**What does it remember if it leaves and rejoins?**
Everything from the same meeting. Rejoin preserves the session, so context carries over and the meeting still shows as a single record in Meeting history.

**Which languages can it speak?**
Whatever your Superhuman persona is configured for. The per-meeting language picker (beta) lists every language the persona can both speak and understand, and the AI disclosure and chat replies are translated to match.

**Does it work for BDR calls as well as technical evaluations?**
Yes. It is designed to ride along on every motion: BDR-led calls, commercial conversations, and deep technical evaluations, with the engagement mode and briefing tuned per meeting.
