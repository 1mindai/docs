This guide walks through putting your Superhuman on your website. It's the step-by-step companion to the [Website embed & script installation](/configuring/integrations/website-embed) reference — start here, and use that page for field-level detail.

## Before you begin

- A **deployment** exists for the Superhuman you want to launch (see [Create & configure a deployment](/using/deployments/create-and-configure)).
- You can edit your site's HTML, or you have access to your tag manager (e.g., Google Tag Manager).
- You know which **domains** the Superhuman should run on.

## 1. Get your launcher code

In the Console, open the Superhuman, go to **Deployments**, open the deployment, and open the **Launcher Codes** tab. You'll find:

- The **launcher script** — the JavaScript snippet to add to your site.
- A **Testing Link** — a live preview you can use before installing anything.

## 2. Add the script to your site

Install the launcher one of two ways:

- **Directly in the page** — paste the launcher script into your site's `<head>` (or just before the closing `</body>`), on the pages where the Superhuman should appear.
- **Via a tag manager** — add the launcher as a custom HTML tag in Google Tag Manager (or similar) and publish. Confirm your tag-manager consent and firing rules load the script where you expect.

## 3. Authorize your domains

In the deployment's **Launch Settings → Access**, add every production, staging, and preview domain that should be allowed to run the launcher. Use exact domains where possible; use `*` only when running on every domain is intentional.

## 4. Test before you launch

Open the **Testing Link** (or your staged page) and confirm the widget appears in the right place, shows your branding, and starts a conversation. If it doesn't appear, walk through [Widget & embed placement troubleshooting](/configuring/integrations/widget-placement-troubleshooting) — the quickest check is confirming the `launcher.1mind.com` script is present in the page's DOM.

## 5. Promote to production

When the deployment is verified, promote it to Production from the deployment editor, then confirm the live page one more time before sharing.

<Warning>Promoting a deployment takes effect immediately and doesn't promote the Superhuman's Brain or knowledge — that's a separate release. See [Release Management](/using/deployments/release-management).</Warning>
