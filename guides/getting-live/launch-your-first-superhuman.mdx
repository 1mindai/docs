When you're ready to stand up a new Superhuman, your 1mind Services team handles provisioning and configuration. The build goes fastest when you have the inputs below ready up front. This page is the checklist of what to prepare and what the process looks like from your side.

## What you provide

Gather these before the build kicks off:

**The essentials**

- **Website URL** — the site the Superhuman will represent and, usually, be embedded on.
- **Use case** — what this Superhuman is for (e.g., website BDR, inbound SDR, solutions engineering). This determines the starting template.
- **Superhuman/assistant name and persona preferences** — the customer-facing name, and appearance/voice preferences (see [Personas](/using/deployments/personas)).

**Branding**

- **Primary brand colors** (a few values for gradient control) and **font** — from your site or brand kit.
- **Logo** and a **light or dark UI theme** preference.
- Optional brand-kit overrides (surface/panel background, chat bubble and text colors).

**Content and messaging**

- **Intro / welcome message** and any **suggested topics** (conversation-starter chips).
- **CTA button text** and any **action buttons** the Superhuman should offer (book a meeting, open a demo, download).

**Privacy and access**

- **Privacy policy text and URL** — your own, for both the widget and the in-experience disclaimer (this replaces any placeholder 1mind text).
- **Allowed domains** — the domains the widget is authorized to run on (or confirm it should run everywhere).

**Knowledge to seed**

- The **website to crawl** and any **documents** the Superhuman should learn from (see [Knowledge Hub](/configuring/knowledge/overview)).

**People and sign-off**

- Who **approves** the build on your side, and which **admin users** should have console access.
- Optional preferences: ZoomInfo enrichment on/off, multilingual support, and any UTM tracking parameters.

## What the process looks like

<Steps>
  <Step title="Kickoff & intake">
    Your CSM confirms the use case and collects the inputs above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provisioning">
    1mind provisions your account, workspace, persona, and a Superhuman from the appropriate template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Training & configuration">
    The Superhuman's Brain (training blocks) is filled in with your company context, and a deployment is built with your branding, experience, privacy policy, and launch settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test">
    You get a **Testing Link** — a live preview — to verify branding, the widget template and position, that the privacy policy shows your name, that chat works, and that the welcome message and any action buttons behave as expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote to production">
    Once approved, the deployment is promoted to Production. After promotion, production configuration is read-only; to iterate, changes are made in a Sandbox copy, tested, and promoted again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>The Superhuman's **Brain and knowledge** and each **deployment** are promoted to Production separately — see [Release Management](/using/deployments/release-management) and [Create & configure a deployment](/using/deployments/create-and-configure).</Note>
